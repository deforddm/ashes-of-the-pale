# Ashes of the Pale v3.17.0: Vell, and levels that count

Shipped Thu Oct 8, 2026. Built on v3.16.1, which made levels add damage and armour and made Rally and the heals grow.

## What David decided (Oct 8, 3:26–3:30 PM PDT)
- Every skill and spell grows with level. Munitions stay the same.
- Stat bumps happen automatically, by role.
- Vell can be recruited, and fights with a ranged grappling hook that pulls an enemy into melee range.
- Only one recruit at a time. If Ellis is in the squad, she can leave to search for Toc so Vell can join. If she went into the rift after Toc, Vell is free to join, which fits the timeline.
- Fix the Ch4 choice labelled only "—".

## Levels
| Level | Gain |
|---|---|
| Every level | +4 health, +1 to hit (unchanged) |
| 3, 5, 7 | +1 damage on every blow (v3.16.1) |
| 4, 8 | +1 armour (v3.16.1) |
| 4 | +1 to the role's first Measure; strain limit 7; longer reach (tier 1) |
| 5 | Longer Veils and Andii dark; +1 use of every chapter trick (including the chapter you're in) |
| 6 | Rally twice a fight; Shield Bash, Grappling Hook recharge faster; Quick Shot fires three; talents +1 (Shield Wall, Hold the Line, Sapper's Eye, Ghost Step) |
| 7 | Strain limit 8; tier 2 for reach, duration and boss resistance |
| 8 | +1 to the role's second Measure; Marine Discipline +1 |

Helpers (src/15_state.js): `lvB()` levels past the first; `lvDmg()`; `lvAc()`; `lvT()` tier (+1 at 4 and 7); `lvAt(n)`; `lvStat(id, stat)`; `lvlNote()` is the level-up note. `strMax()` (14_data.js) replaces the old `STR_MAX` constant.

Measure by role (`STATUP`, 14_data.js), level 4 then level 8:
- Sergeant: Wits, then Might
- Brisk: Might, then Wits
- Kettle: Wits, then Might
- Tuft: Guile, then Wits
- Ohl: Wits, then Might
- Ellis: Guile, then Wits
- Vell: Guile, then Might

Skill checks are about one point easier for the soldier who rolls them by level 8. The DCs were left alone. Nudge Ch5–7 DCs if checks feel too easy.

What grows:
- **Heals and Rally:** Rally, Mend, Denul Wash, Stanch (now heals a little), salves, and the Rhivi Spirit-Song.
- **Attacks:** Shield Bash.
- **Spells:** Veil (penalty and length), Phantom (turns), and Mockra (boss resist).
- **Talents and abilities:** Tracker's Mark (damage), Quick Shot, Shadow Step (reach), Argument with Hood, and Vell's hook and Rope Swing.
- **Tricks:**
  - the Line (armour)
  - the Rope's Way (reach)
  - Hand-Cant (one more critical hit at tiers 1 and 2)
  - Blue Fire (damage)
  - Otataral (reach)
  - Andii dark (length)
  - Rime (damage, boss resist)
  - chapter-trick uses

Unchanged: sharpers, burners, cussers, smokers, and the Quorl Signal's sharper.

## Vell
- **Sheet** (TPL.vell): Rope man · Guild journeyman.
  - Combat: 15 health, armour 13, +4 to hit, a knife at 1d6+1, move 6, initiative +4.
  - Measure: Might 1, Wits 1, Guile 3.
- **Kit:** Grappling Hook and Salve.
  - The hook is thrown at 2 to 3 tiles (4 at level 4, 5 at level 7) for 1d6+1+level damage, and hauls the target to the open tile beside Vell that is nearest it.
  - Bosses, immortals and anything with 45+ health take the damage but stay put.
  - It recharges after 2 rounds (1 from level 6).
- **Level-3 talents:**
  - Rope Swing: a free move once a turn, to any open tile within 3+ that draws no free swings.
  - Snare: whatever the hook hauls in is −2 to hit and −2 armour through its next turn.
- **God:** Cotillion, the Rope, the same as Ellis. They can never both be in the squad. His intervention uses Cotillion's sigil.
- **Portrait** (19_portraits.js, POR.vell): black curls, soot, Ohl's stitches on the cheek, the tarred coil and the three-tined hook.
- **When he joins:**
  - **Ch4** (shield path), at the blue lamp after he gives the token (`c4_vell_thanks`): "Come with us."
    - Without Ellis, he joins (`c4_vell_join`).
    - With Ellis, she offers to go east to find Toc (`c4_vell_ellis`). Then either "Go and find him, Ellis" (`c4_ellis_toc`: `letGo('ellis')`, `S.f.c4_ellisToc`, Vell joins), or "Not tonight, Vell" (`c4_vell_later`).
  - **Ch6**, under the crossing (`c6_vell`), if he was shielded, isn't in the squad, and nobody holds the sixth place (Ellis went into the grey, or was never taken). He comes down the ladder-hole on his rope; Whiskeyjack says "Your count, Sergeant."
- **One recruit at a time:**
  - In Ch7, Ellis back from the grey can't be offered her place while Vell has it (`c7_ellis_back2` and `c7_ellis_walk`). She goes her own way.
  - In Ch4 she says it plainly: "Six is what the Fourth carries."
- **His lines:**
  - **Ch4:** the corner man; the dawn debrief (Whiskeyjack counts six and meets him); the roof summary; a close (`c4_close_vell`).
  - **Ch5:** the ladder; Crone; Toc on Ellis (if she went east); Ellis at the rent and her prints at dawn.
  - **Ch6:** the Guild knives on the terrace know him (Harl); a close (`c6_close_vell`).
  - **Ch7:**
    - On the quay, Harl gives the Guild's word to the squad through him (`c7_harl`, which sets `c7_vellWarned`).
    - Close on the quorl hill (`c7_close_vell`).
    - His road on each ending: he follows outlaw at loyalty ≥1, empire at ≥2, city at ≥−1, and never on disband.
    - Fate pages: "Where the line goes."
    - A dead-list page if he falls.
- **Ellis gone east** gets her own finale page ("He kept riding") and a line in the chapter recap and the journal.
- **New name:** Harl, the older Guild man.
