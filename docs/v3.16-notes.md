## What v3.16.0 changed

### Canon

- **Names that clashed with the books.** The gate clerk is **Pennick** (Pallick is a Hound of Shadow); Brisk's brother is **Tavrin**, called Tav ("Tavore-by-no-relation" is gone); the invented "Chains" card is **the Wain**, Dragnipur's wagon. Internal ids and flags (`pallick`, `c3_pallickDoubt`, card id `chains`) are unchanged, so saves load.
- **The Mhybe and the child.** *Mhybe* is glossed as "a vessel, the kind you fill once and do not keep"; before the ashes the Rhivi say "the clan-mother's fires"; the woman with the bundle is no longer old and is named the Mhybe after the ashes; she carries the bundle herself. The bundle is bigger by Chapter 5, and in Chapter 7 the reborn Tattersail is a child of four or five in red wool, growing in weeks and months, not snows. Paran has met her on the plain, "and whatever is between them will wait".
- **Whiskeyjack's leg** could mend with a week flat on his back; he won't give it the week. Nobody heals it.
- **The High Mage** is never placed in Chapter 7: his staff is at Genabaris, and the badge is "a window".
- **The outlawing**: the narrator no longer vouches for it ("sounded as if it meant it").
- **The Claw in the Host**: the rolls list "Claw, attached" as Toc alone; the grey cloak's people came into the Pale camp after the city fell and are not the Host's.
- **The Fete** as the book has it: the tall guest leaves before the Tyrant (his glass untouched on the rail); the dragon rises off an old bell-tower up the hill; the demon dies behind the walls of the Estate District; the five dragons are coming back hurt, not setting out; Rallick wears a cheap tiger mask; Blues is squat, with a plain sword in each hand and no sorcery, and Lorn breaks off "very nearly running"; Ocelot's "standing word" replaces his foreknowledge of the Fete.
- **Spans and places**: Coll's fall and Rallick's wait are two years; Rallick is "long in the leg"; Kettle's cusser dates "since Nathilog"; Kalam has watched the Andii "every night since we came"; "a month" became "all these weeks" where Pale to Chapter 5 was meant; the Daru tongue is "Daru", not "Daric"; "Tuesday" became "Market day"; the Lakefront knife watches "the warehouses", not "the Council's houses".
- **Directions**: Tool walks south of the road; Ellis rides "South, some days; east, most"; Moon's Spawn goes west at first light, then "Somewhere south, over water", and Crone follows it south; the empire road runs north-west, wide of Pale; the Host waits outside Pale; there is no winter camp.
- **Lore**: Crone's Jaghut "went away" (not "died out"); the Empire "had" an army of T'lan Imass; otataral sits on whoever holds it (Tuft feels it "like a tooth") and is mined at the edge of the Otataral Desert; the Mouse Quarter purge was the new mage cadre, not the Claw; Bellurdan was cadre and carried Nightchill's body out of Pale; Tattersail and Bellurdan lie "close enough to be one"; Kettle "Ran contraband", and her ledger counts "every one"; Tool's sword is flint, no colour; Kruppe takes Tuft to the Phoenix "whenever he is in the city"; Kalam's Ch4 dawn word is "Moon's Spawn", so Rake stays unnamed in the chapters; Paran "gave it away" (Chance).
- **Rules**: the Elder tricks (A Courtesy of Darkness, Omtose Rime) work beside otataral; Soliel is "Mistress of Healing"; the glossary's Onearm's Host is "the Second, Fifth and Sixth".
- **The seven Deck visions** had their canon pass: Hood's Gate of bodies, Paran naming Chance himself, seven warrens in the pillar of fire, Quick Ben escorted by Hounds, the warehouse fight on the roof, the pack at Hairlock's end with Rake speaking, Rake's kill at Baruk's and his words to Crokus, Lorn's Finnest as an acorn, Blues with two blades, "Fete" spelled the house way.

## Open questions for David

1. **The cold-badge loyalty call.** A squad that drives Tuft to `loy <= -2` reaches the grey cloak's cold route; if Rake has already cut her badge, that route now ends in the dark cut, and the dark cut gives her +2 loyalty like any cut. Keep the +2, or give that route a smaller number of its own?
2. **"Jammit's Worry"** or the map's **"Jatem's Worry"**? The game says Jammit's Worry everywhere, on one fan source. Check your copy of the novel's glossary or map, and it is a find-and-replace.
3. **Moon's Spawn before the Fete**: over the lake (as now), or south of the city as one wiki line has it? Moving it changes the Ch3 and Ch6 skies, several lines and the art.
4. **Raest's ice and otataral.** The squad's Elder tricks now ignore otataral, but otataral dust still stops the Tyrant's Omtose Phellack lance (`31_battle.js:401`). Make Raest immune too, for consistency, or keep it as the one thing the squad can do about him?
5. **The Kettle name clash.** The later books have Kettle, the undead child of the Azath in Letheras, and the game already sends an Azath root toward Kettle's scorch in the Fete garden. Keep the name (the current decision), or rename her before a sequel reaches Lether?

## What's new text

For the lead to paste into the game as the 3.16.0 note:

- The story has been checked against the novels, line by line, and set right wherever it strayed.
- A few names have changed: the clerk at the Worry Gate is Pennick, Brisk's brother is Tavrin, and the card once called Chains is now the Wain.
- The maps of Genabackis and Darujhistan now follow the lie of the land in the books.
- Tuft remembers what she has seen in the Deck's visions.
- The painted set is finished: every card in Tuft's Deck, a painting at the end of every chapter, and one picture at each chapter's opening.
- The two oldest tricks the squad can learn now work even beside otataral, as the books say they should.
- What's new shows everything since you last played. The journal keeps notes to the end of the book, and its glossary grows as you meet things.
- Deeds now count your tricks, the gods' answers and the visions you have seen.
- Bigger buttons, clearer small text, curly apostrophes, and updates that download once.
