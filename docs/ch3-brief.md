## What Chapter 3 is

The Fourth comes down out of the Gadrobi Hills into Darujhistan, the city of blue fire, with the Bridgeburners' baggage wagon — a day late if `S.f.c2_late`, on time otherwise. The Bridgeburners are already under the city posing as a road crew hired to repair a Gadrobi District intersection, and are in fact packing Moranth munitions into the gas conduits beneath the crossroads. Canon fixed points, all of which happen *near* the Fourth and none of which they can change: Whiskeyjack runs the crew and is counting days; Fiddler and Hedge are down the hole with the crates; Mallet patches; Trotts stands at the barrier and is enormous; Sorry stands apart and everyone in the squad is afraid of her without saying so (Fiddler: "Don't talk to her. Don't look at her long enough for her to notice."); Quick Ben and Kalam are gone into the city by night on a business of their own (making contact with the Assassins' Guild — the Fourth hears the words *the Guild* once and is told to forget them); Moon's Spawn hangs over the lake, black, silent, and nobody in the city looks up any more; the Phoenix Inn is where the city's thieves drink — Kruppe (fat, in a red waistcoat, talks about himself in the third person and is never, ever wrong), Crokus the young thief, Murillio the dandy, Coll the big drunk who used to be somebody; Rallick Nom is a name said quietly, not a man met (Chapter 4 is his); Baruk the alchemist is a name on a coach door. Nobody canon dies or lives because of the player.

**The choice that matters:** the Claw has a house in Darujhistan (Ellis said so at the edge of the hills). On the second night a message reaches the sergeant — through Ellis if she is in the squad (a boy she knew from the Claw's training yards finds her, and she brings it to you, and is not happy about it), through a street urchin with a coin and a written address otherwise. A Claw contact in the Daru District — **Madryn**, an original: a grey-haired woman who keeps a dye-shop and has kept it for eleven years, gentle-voiced, terrifying — wants to know what the Bridgeburners are doing under the intersection, and what Kalam and Quick Ben are doing at night. She offers silver and the Empire's regard; she mentions the sergeant's name, which she should not know (`S.f.marked` — she has the file). **Report** = tell her (as much or as little as the player chooses, but the key is set): `S.f.c3_key='report'`, `S.silver += 40`, `S.f.clawFavour=1`, Brisk −2 ("that's the squad's sergeant, Sergeant"), Ellis −2 if present (this is her test, and you failed it), Tuft +1 (she likes a sergeant who keeps a door open), Ohl −1; Madryn's knives leave the Fourth alone; Whiskeyjack never finds out this chapter, but `S.f.c3_told=1` is a loaded gun for later. **Refuse** = walk out (or lie to her — Guile 14 for a clean lie, which she may or may not believe: `S.f.c3_lied=1`), and Madryn's hired Daru knives come for the Fourth in the alley behind the dye-shop that same night (`startBattle('knives')`): `S.f.c3_key='refuse'`, `S.f.marked=1` (if it wasn't already), Brisk +1, Ellis +2 if present, `S.f.wjRegard += 1` if the player tells Whiskeyjack afterwards (a node at the dig: he listens, says nothing for a long moment, and then: "Good." That's all. It's a lot, from him). Either way the chapter ends at dawn on a rooftop above the dig with Moon's Spawn over the lake and the blue fire going out lamp by lamp, and the first of the rooftop shapes the tease is about.

Also honour `S.f.c2_late`: at the first meeting Whiskeyjack says the day out loud ("Eight days. I said seven.") and `S.f.wjRegard -= 1`; if not late, "Seven days." and nothing, which from him is praise. If `S.f.c2_key === 'light'` he has heard about the light (Crone told Rake, Rake told... no: he *hasn't* heard, and doesn't ask, and Tuft watches him not ask). If `S.f.c2_croneSaw`, Quick Ben, when he turns up, knows the Great Raven spoke to the sergeant and says so once, lightly, in a way that isn't light.
