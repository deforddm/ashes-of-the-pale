The road through Gardens of the Moon

Chapter Plates

One plate for each chapter opening, from the dark under Pale to the Lakefront at dawn.

Prologue
Varrow's Journal

Under the Pale, in the dark: a dead man's journal and one underlined line.

Chapter I
Pale

Three days after the fall: the cadre row, the burial field, and Hounds in the camp at night.

Chapter II
The Rhivi Plain

Open grass from day to night, a tracker named Ellis, and something old watching from above.

Chapter III
Blue Fire

Darujhistan: gaslight, the Phoenix Inn, and a dig under the Gadrobi crossing.

Chapter IV
Assassins

The rooftops at night, Kalam's meet, the Tiste Andii, and a choice on the roof.

Chapter V
The Gadrobi Hills

Lorn and Tool, a barrow, Hairlock's rent, and the Hounds in the grey.

Chapter VI
The Fete

Masks at Lady Simtal's gate, a vault under the Gadrobi crossing, and a Tyrant at the party.

Chapter VII
Outlaws

Dawn on the Lakefront, an Empire that has outlawed its own army, and a sky with nothing in it.
