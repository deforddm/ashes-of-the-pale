Story bible · section 5

Motifs & Voice

The things the game says again and again, until they mean more than they did the first time.

Counting

Everyone counts the Fourth. Brisk counts rations, Kettle munitions, Ohl his list.

“Arrives at five, and checks it.”

Ledgers

The Claw's neat hand, Brisk's rations, the pay ledger, Ohl's list, the green door. The same idea, every time.

“Ledgers are where names go to be found, Sergeant.”

Pell the mule

Same face as the quartermaster. Bites Gadrobi. In the ledger.

“I miss the mule.”

Ohl's tea

Technically medicine. Dujek says it's like boiled boots. Ohl has a rule.

“Is that medicine?” “Technically.”

We don't leave people

Brisk's, from Chapter 5. Carried out to Whiskeyjack. Answered by Ellis.

“That's the half nobody says.”

Clean boots

Clean boots mean the Claw. The grey cloak's last act is to look at his wet ones.

Nobody else on the road has them.

A pause for a knife

When the prose leaves a pause you could fit a knife into, that's Kalam.

“You didn't see that. Nobody sees that.”

It's the gas

Darujhistan's cover story for every sapper's noise. Tunnels carry. A lane's a barrel.

“It's the gas. It's usually the gas.”

Hounds remember

The hold-down lines in Chapters 1 and 5 come back at the Fete. Shadow is where they went home.

“It knew me.”

Polite

Tuft's word. First Shadowthrone's answer, then her own mouth at the Fete, then her fate page.

“It was polite.”
