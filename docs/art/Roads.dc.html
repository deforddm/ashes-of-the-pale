The Four Roads

On the bench, Whiskeyjack asks, and the sergeant tells him. All four roads are always open. Who walks them with you is another matter.

Outlaws
You flew north

With the Host the Empress has outlawed. Attached to the Bridgeburners, if Whiskeyjack trusts you; with the baggage if not, and Paran asked for you anyway.

The first thing the Empire has ever called the Fourth that it meant.

Loyalists
You went home

Across the sea to the Empire, pardon or no pardon. The Claw's page stays open, or is closed, or is drowned.

You carry it across the sea like ballast.

Citizens
You stayed

Deserters, in a city that keeps its own books. Kruppe arranged it. If the Worry Gate has a new crack in its arch, you use another gate.

Darujhistan does not read the Empire's ledger.

Stood Down
You stood the Fourth down

There's no form for it. Each leaves by their own road, and nobody follows anybody.

The squad ends. The people don't.

Who follows
