Munitions & supplies
Sharper
Moranth · thrown · Kettle

A clay egg the size of a fist that breaks into a hundred knives. Kettle counts them like prayers.

Burner
Moranth · fire · Kettle

Liquid fire in a waxed jar. Strapped down near otataral, and nobody stands downwind.

Cusser
Moranth · crossbow cradle · Kettle

Chub's, and her name is Maud. Kettle keeps her out of ordinary fights.

Smoker
Moranth · from Hedge's cellar · Ch 3

A grey jar that blooms into a wall. No shot goes into the smoke or out of it.

Phial of acid
Sapper's timer · the Ch 6 vault

Stoppered, carried upright, with a tin of tallow for wax plugs. Acid and wax instead of fuse-cord.

Salve
Field dressing · anyone can apply it

One to start, more from Quartermaster Pell, one found each chapter from the Rhivi Plain on. Ellis rations her own for the hand.
