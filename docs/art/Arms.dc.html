The pack · picked up along the way

Weapons & Armour

Twelve pieces of kit, Chapter One to the Fete. Most of them belonged to someone who no longer needs them.

Weapons
Grey cloak's knife
Ch 1 · +1 attack · Sergeant, Kettle

No maker's mark and no blood on it, which is not the same as never having had any.

Rhivi horn bow
Ch 2 · +1 attack, +1 range · Ellis, Kettle

Horn and sinew, unstrung and given. Sethand said it was a bad bow. He did not say whose.

Daru duelling knife
Ch 3 · +1 attack · Sergeant, Ellis

A basket of brass wire round the grip. He had not stolen it recently; he had stolen it well.

Guild blade
Ch 4 · +1 attack · Sergeant, Ellis, Kettle

Lamp-soot black, the edge left bright. Made to be carried up a drainpipe in the teeth, and it has been.

Barrow-flint blade
Ch 5 · +1 attack · all but Tuft, Ohl

Knapped so fine the edge is translucent, bound to antler with sinew older than the Empire's name.

House guard's halberd
Ch 6 · +1 attack · Brisk, Sergeant

Polished for show, sharpened by somebody who did not trust the show. Brisk calls it a hat you hold.

Blacked Guild blade
Ch 6 · +1 attack, +1 Guile · Sergeant, Ellis, Kettle

Soot-black from point to pommel, so not even the edge catches a lantern. Made for a party.

Armour
Second Army heater
Ch 1 · +1 armour · Brisk, Sergeant

The Second's sigil burned off. Brisk did not ask whose.

Toc's spare cloak
Ch 2 · +1 armour · Ellis, Tuft, Sergeant

Grey going brown, the shoulder cut for a bow. Toc left it on the tailboard and did not look back.

Road-crew leather
Ch 3 · +1 armour · anyone

The Paviors' mark burned into the back. Smells of gas and wet stone. Fits nobody and everybody.

Andii-grey cloak
Ch 4 · +1 armour · Tuft

The grey of ash on a cold hearth. It does not quite take the lamplight.

Scout's cloak
Ch 5 · +1 armour · Ellis, Kettle, Sergeant

Toc's, rolled tight with a scout's knot. The horse came back without him before dawn.
