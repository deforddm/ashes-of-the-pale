Onearm's Host · marines

The Fourth

Five marines up out of the dark under Pale, and a Claw scout who chose the road. Everyone counts them.

The Sergeant
Hask, unless you say otherwise
Unta · patron Fener

Eleven years a marine, three under Dujek. Keeps the pay ledger. Carries a Second Army whistle no longer used, and a knife nobody has seen drawn.

“Arrives at five, and checks it.”

Brisk
Corporal · heavy infantry
Cawn · patron Hood

Her brother Tav joined the Second a year before her. She checks the pits when nobody is watching, and carries his letter, three years old, still sealed. She has never prayed. She has always saluted.

“We don't leave people.”

Kettle
Sapper · Moranth munitions
Falar · patron Oponn

Ran powder for a smuggler; learned munitions from Chub, who lost three fingers. Owes a Moranth quartermaster something she won't name. Carries a spoon she will not explain.

“Two sharpers, one burner, one cusser. Same as this morning.”

Tuft
Squad mage · Meanas
Malaz City · patron Shadowthrone

A foundling. A season on the High Mage's staff, then out, with a letter that said nothing. Goes very still when someone says “High Mage.” Never draws a card for herself. Sleeps with a lamp lit.

“It was polite.”

Ohl
Healer · Denul
Ehrlitan · patron Soliel

A temple healer who served the Second under Dujek. Carries the list: oilcloth, two hundred and eleven names. He has not added one from the Fourth yet, and intends to die before he does.

“Is that medicine?” “Technically.”

Ellis
Scout · Claw-trained
Genabaris · patron Cotillion

Six years a Claw scout; Toc's scout. Burned her hand at Pale pulling a courier out of a tent. Wears a Claw whistle with the cord cut. Says one sentence, and it's the right one.

“Fire at the Pale. Before the Hounds.”
