The pack · worn close

Trinkets

Fifteen small things that do not weigh much. Every one of them was somebody's, and most of them still are.

Hound's tooth
Ch 1 · +1 attack · anyone

Snapped off in a tent pole, the length of a finger. It is warm. It stays warm.

Cadre token
Ch 1 · +2 health · Tuft, Ohl

The kind the cadre give to people they mean to remember. Tattersail did not say whose it had been.

Barrow torc
Ch 2 · +2 health · anyone

Black iron from a dead man's neck, colder than it should be. Tuft would not touch it.

Second Army badge
Ch 2 · +1 armour · Brisk, Sergeant

The Ninth Regiment's. Tav was the Fourth. Brisk knows the difference and carries it anyway.

Blue-glass lamp-chip
Ch 3 · +1 Might, Wits, Guile · anyone

Daru knives wear them for luck. This one's luck ran out, which some would say makes it due.

Coll's old signet
Ch 3 · +1 Guile · anyone

The device ground off; only the shape of a house is left. It was worth a council seat once. It bought a round.

Guild token
Ch 4 · +1 Guile · anyone

Black horn, nothing carved on it at all. Vell says it opens a door. He does not say which.

Rope-and-hook
Ch 4 · +1 move · Ellis, Sergeant, Kettle

Thirty feet of tarred line, the hook wrapped in rag so it does not ring on slate. The journeyman will not need it.

Rhivi bone charm
Ch 5 · +1 Wits · anyone

A horse's knuckle-bone and one blue bead, so the grass will know whose you are. Sethand tied it on himself.

Oponn's Own Coin
Ch 3 · +1 Guile · anyone

A copper with a head on both sides. Has never lost a toss. Doctor Brack calls that policy.

Hood's Repellent
Ch 3 · once a chapter, stay up at 1 · anyone

Water, tar and possibly a beetle. No customer has ever come back to complain. Ohl will not say it doesn't work.

Genuine Shard of Moon's Spawn
Ch 3 · nothing at all · anyone

Forge slag in a velvet bag, with a certificate in the doctor's own hand. Kettle keeps it next to the cussers.

Map to the Tyrant's Hoard
Ch 3 · nothing at all · anyone

Sea-serpents in the margins of a country with no sea, and an X in the Gadrobi Hills. Not to scale.

Otataral-dusted glove
Ch 5 · +1 armour · Sergeant, Brisk

Left where the Adjunct's tent stood. Red dust in the seams; sorcery slides off it like rain off oilcloth.

Fete mask
Ch 6 · +1 Guile · anyone

Dark red silk with tiny brass bells stuffed with wax so they will not ring. Murillio bought too many.

Sapper's satchel strap
Ch 6 · +2 health · anyone

Hedge's spare, the buckle filed smooth so it will not strike a spark. For something close to the ribs.

Moranth chit
Ch 7 · +2 health · Kettle

Black chitin with one notch. The Moranth give them to those they have finished counting with.

A key to no door
Ch 7 · +1 Guile · anyone

Opens the Phoenix's back door, the one that is not there. Kruppe forgets which copies he gave to whom.

A neat hand's pen
Ch 7 · +1 Wits · anyone

Left on the Lakefront the morning a grey cloak's book was closed, by a man who never put anything down.
