The Original Cast

The people Ashes of the Pale added to the world. None of them are in the books. All of them keep some kind of ledger.

The grey cloak
Claw · Prologue to Ch 7

Clean boots. A very neat hand. A very good memory for sergeants.

“Three rounds, Sergeant. You held them. That's been noted.”

Madryn
Claw handler · the dye-shop · Ch 3

Blue-dyed hands, a pot of tea, and your name in her best hand.

“I'll sit here and think about you.”

Vell
Guild journeyman · Ch 4, 7

Ocelot's clan. A rope man, and a black horn token that opens a door.

“Vell. Journeyman. I owe you.”

Ch'kess
Black Moranth quartermaster · Ch 7

Two clicks and a hiss. Carries both rolls, the living and the dead.

“Spent is square.”

Pennick
Gate clerk · the Worry Gate · Ch 3, 7

Writes “goat” in his margin when he doubts you. Keep to the right-hand side of the arch.

“I keep a column.”

Sethand
Rhivi guide · the clan-mother's people · Ch 2, 5, 7

Sings over the dead horses, and gives the words to those he trusts.

“Come by the clan-mother's fires. Say my name.”

Moreau
Deserter of the Second · Prologue, Ch 2

Garrow's man in the tunnels, and on a ridge at dusk. Never resolved.

“That's twice. Tell Garrow. Or don't.”

Quartermaster Pell
The Host's stores · Ch 1

Has one face and one word for everything. The mule is named for the face.

“Adequate.” · “Off the books. The books never liked you.”

Ocelot's man · Ch 4, 6, 7

“Ocelot sends his regards.”
