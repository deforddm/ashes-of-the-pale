In-World Papers

Three papers for the journal or chapter ends: Brisk's ration ledger, a Claw file in a neat hand, and the proclamation that makes outlaws of them all.

Rations · the Fourth
Eighteen days hardtack, five. Fourteen if the mule eats. Eleven if Kettle does. Mule's Pell. One day lost, west, on the sergeant's order. Ten silver, to deserters, for the road. Ten silver. Alley. In line before they stood. One vault. Forty and twelve. Not fired.
Kept by Brisk, corporal V
File · not for the rolls

Unit. Fourth squad, marines, Onearm's Host.

Noted. Came up out of the Pale with all five. Somebody has noticed.

Sergeant. Untan. Keeps the pay ledger. Carries a knife nobody has seen drawn.

Mage. Cadre. Once on the High Mage's staff. Transferred with a letter that said nothing.

Sapper. Falari. One spoon, listed under equipment.

Healer. Keeps a list. Not ours.

Disposition. Watch.

Written in a very neat hand. The boots that carried it were clean.
By the Throne
Proclamation

Let it be known in every city of Genabackis that the army styled Onearm's Host, with every regiment, company and squad under it, is struck from the rolls of the Empire and declared

Outlaw

No pay shall be drawn against its name. No quartermaster shall feed it. No soldier of the Empire shall count it among the living.
