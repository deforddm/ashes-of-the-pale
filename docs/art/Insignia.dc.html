Marks of the road

Signs & Insignia

Small emblems for menus, save slots, loading screens and the backs of cards.

The Fourth

A squad patch, five tallies struck through. Everyone counts them. You count twice.

The Phoenix Inn

The door with the painted bird, in the Daru District. You pay before you drink. Kruppe is at his table.

Guild of Paviors

Burned into the road crew's leather. It smells of gas and wet stone. Pell ends up with them in lieu of wages.

The torn armband

Onearm's Host colours, torn off on the terrace steps at dawn after the Fete. Nobody asked them to. Nobody stopped them.
