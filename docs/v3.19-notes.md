# Ashes of the Pale v3.19.0: sight, smoke and pits

Shipped Fri Oct 9, 2026. Built on v3.18.3.

## What David asked for (Oct 9, 10:21 PM PDT)
- Draw the smoker's smoke as one cloud, like A Courtesy of Darkness.
- Fix the game hanging for a bit when smoke is thrown.
- Improve line of sight wherever it matters. Things like tables block movement but not sight; pillars block both; pits are different again.
- As a bonus, let Vell's hook, Shield Drive and other forced movement send someone into a pit, unless they save and go prone on a nearby tile.

## The hang
- **Cause:** a smoker is an area throw, so `useAb` waits for `blast()` to hand the turn back. A smoker never calls `blast()`, so the fight sat busy until the watchdog freed it a few seconds later.
- **Fix:** `smoker.run` now calls `updBattleUI(); afterAct()` when the jar lands. The turn comes back in about 60 ms.
- The other area abilities were already fine: the sharper, burner, cusser, Quorl and Blue Fire all use `blast()`, and the Andii dark calls `afterAct` itself.

## Smoke as one cloud
- Each smoker's tiles share a group id (`g`), as the dark does.
- One drawing routine now does both: a radial cloud clipped to its tiles, drifting wisps, and a dashed edge.
- Smoke is grey and drifts faster; the dark is violet-black and slow.
- Ungrouped smoke (`preSmoke`, the Ch6 row) is grouped by when it expires.

## Ground (31_battle.js)
| Glyph | What | Walk | See / shoot | Forced into it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#` | Walls, pillars, standing stones | No | Blocks | Stops it: Shield Drive's +1d6 slam |
| `t` | Tables, crates, fallen stones, drying frames | No | Clear; +2 armour against shots if it's right in front of the target | Stops it, like a wall |
| `o` | Pits and shafts | No | Clear | Save or fall |
| `#` on roof maps | The drop between roofs | No | Clear | Save or fall |
| `#` on dock maps | The lake | No | Clear | Save or fall |

Helpers:
- `tileCh(x,y)` returns the glyph at a tile.
- `solid()` blocks sight.
- `lowAt()` and `pitAt()` identify low cover and pits.
- `wall()` now means "nobody stands here" (`#`, `t` or `o`), so `free()`, pathing, reach and spawning keep working unchanged.
- Smoke and the dark spread over anything that isn't `solid`, so a table can't leave a hole in a cloud.

## Line of sight
- `sees(a, b)` traces the line between the two tile centres and is blocked by any `solid` tile in between.
  - It's lenient at the edges: a line from either side of the shooter's tile also counts (±0.3 of a tile), so you can lean round a pillar.
  - A line straight through a pillar, or a pillar on the diagonal, still blocks.
  - Adjacent tiles always see each other.
- `canShoot(a, t)` now requires both `sees` and no smoke anywhere on the line, including both ends. Before, it only checked the two end tiles.
- `coverFor(a, t)`: a `t` tile on the line and next to the target gives +2 armour against shots. It's not checked for free swings.
- The log names the reason: "out of range", "no line of sight" or "smoke in the way". Attack tags show "behind cover" and "prone".
- Needing sight is now part of every aimed ability:
  - Shots and anything using `canShoot`: ranged attacks, Tracker's Mark, Quick Shot, the hook, enemy shooters and spells, posted-up shots and the enemy daze.
  - Veil, Phantom, Mend, Stanch, Mockra, Rope Swing, Hand-Cant, Otataral and Rime.
  - Throws and the Andii dark: you must be able to see the tile you aim at (`aoeOk`).
- Exempt:
  - Quorl (`sky:true`) drops from the sky.
  - Shadow Step and the Rope's Way go through Meanas.
  - Rally, Denul Wash and Argument with Hood don't need sight.

## Pits and edges
`overEdge(t, pit, how)` is called by `forcedMove` (the hook and Shield Drive) when the next step is a pit. Shield Drive's path now stops in a pit instead of slamming against it. The enemy shove rider (31c) calls it too.

The save is the same as fire's: d20 + the better of Might and Guile (+2 if Steady) against 12 + the difficulty for the squad, or d20 + half the to-hit against 12 for anyone else.
- **Made:** it catches the lip and goes prone on the tile it was on (the nearby tile next to the pit).
  - Prone means no move on its next turn: the squad's existing rule, and new for enemies via `u.rising` and `aiWalk`.
  - Blades next to it get −2 armour against it.
- **Failed, enemy:** it goes into the pit and is out of the fight. It counts as a kill, and its body dwindles away down the drop instead of falling over. Bosses, immortals and anything with 45+ health are never moved, as before.
- **Failed, squadmate or ally:** they climb back up hurt (2d6+2) and prone.
- The pit is named in the log: `def.pitTxt`, otherwise "the drop" on the roofs and "the lake" on the quays.
