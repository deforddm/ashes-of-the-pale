Its numbering was set to agree with every beat ID the bible cites (§8 items 43–58 and the §9 canon-anchors table); where a chapter has more scenes than the bible's numbering allows, neighbouring scenes are merged into one beat and the merge is noted.

**What it is.** One line per beat of Steven Erikson's *Gardens of the Moon* (1999), in book order: where, who, what happens. Written in my own words from scene summaries; nothing is quoted from the novel or the articles. Nobody who built this had the novel's text, so the summaries are the evidence.

## Prologue (Malaz City, 1154 BS)

- **P.1** Mock's Hold wall above the Mouse Quarter. Ganoes Paran, twelve, waits while his father does business with the Fist inside; below, the Quarter is being purged by sorcery.
- **P.2** Same. A Bridgeburner commander (Whiskeyjack, unnamed in the scene per W) joins the boy; they talk of the First Sword Dassem Ultor, reported dead at Y'Ghatan.
- **P.3** Same. A second soldier, carrying a fiddle, joins them; talk of the purge and of Surly having taken the name Laseen. *(W; R does not mention the second soldier.)*
- **P.4** Same. Surly arrives with Claw acolytes; she and the commander trade veiled threats about Kellanved's return and her ban on sorcery; she tells him his troops ship out soon, and the fiddler is sent down into the Quarter.
- **P.5** Same. Paran says he will be a soldier; the commander warns him off and the boy scoffs.

## Chapter 1 (Itko Kan and Unta, 1161 BS)

- **1.1** Itko Kan coast road. The old seer Rigga (Riggalai) grabs a fishergirl as cavalry pass, prophesies over her, and is killed by a rider.
- **1.2** Same. Ammanas (Shadowthrone) and Cotillion take the girl for their revenge on Laseen; Cotillion sends seven Hounds of Shadow after the troop.
- **1.3** The massacre site. Adjunct Lorn finds a cavalry regiment and the villagers torn apart; she judges it sorcery and a diversion, and takes Lieutenant Ganoes Paran onto her staff.
- **1.4** A recruiting post. The fishergirl enlists as "Sorry" and asks for Onearm's Host on Genabackis; a staff sergeant (Aragan) notes the wrong-coloured mud on her boots. *(Aragan from W.)*
- **1.5** Gerrom. Paran finds the town empty, the constabulary dead under a blanket of black pigeons, and the recruit records gone.
- **1.6** Road to Unta, the Imperial Warren, the throne room. Topper escorts Paran through the warren; his horse breaks the mosaic; Laseen remembers him from Mock's Hold.
- **1.7** Unta. Lorn gives him a few days before he joins her; at the family estate he meets his sister Tavore (Felisin is at her studies, their father ailing).

## Chapter 2 (Pale, 1163 BS)

- **2.1** The hill outside Pale after the battle with Moon's Spawn. Tattersail and the half-body of Hairlock survive; Bridgeburners (Whiskeyjack, Quick Ben, Kalam, Sorry) come up and say most of the regiment was buried when the tunnels under Pale came down. *(The figure of about 1,400 buried is the bible's [?].)*
- **2.2** Flashback, the planning council. Tattersail, Tayschrenn, Dujek and the Bridgeburners; distrust between the Empress's people and the old guard; Tayschrenn takes command of the strike on Moon's Spawn.
- **2.3** Flashback, the battle. The cadre goes up against the Spawn; A'Karonys and Nightchill die; Calot dies; Tattersail suspects Tayschrenn let the cadre burn. Moon's Spawn draws off south.
- **2.4** Pale. Quick Ben moves Hairlock's mind into a wooden puppet, which goes to Tattersail; she joins the Bridgeburners' scheme for a chance at Tayschrenn.
- **2.5** Bridgeburners alone: talk of the Empress weeding out the old Emperor's people.
- **2.6** Tattersail reads the Deck: the Knight of Darkness and Oponn; she hears a coin spinning.

## Chapter 3 (Genabaris and Pale)

- **3.1** At sea and at Genabaris. Topper gives Paran Whiskeyjack's squad, Sorry's squad, with orders for Darujhistan; Sorry is said to have rotted the Bridgeburners.
- **3.2** Genabaris. Paran learns the Moranth will carry him to Pale by quorl. *(The bible cites 3.2 with 8.1 for Green Moranth quorls; R does not name the clan.)* [?]
- **3.3** Pale. Tattersail meets Bellurdan, who grieves for Nightchill and means to raise her barrow on the Rhivi Plain.
- **3.4** Pale. Whiskeyjack, Kalam and Quick Ben: Laseen is trying to finish them; what is Sorry; a use for Hairlock.
- **3.5** Pale. Tattersail reads the Deck for Tayschrenn and sends word to Whiskeyjack.
- **3.6** Pale. Toc the Younger meets Paran: the soldiers love Dujek and Whiskeyjack; the local Claw master was murdered.
- **3.7** Pale, on the way to the barracks. Paran meets a few Bridgeburners.
- **3.8** Same. Sorry, ridden by Cotillion, kills Paran.
- **3.9** Shadow. Shadowthrone and Cotillion talk of their revenge, and sense something entering their realm.

## Chapter 4 (Pale)

- **4.1** Tattersail with Whiskeyjack, Quick Ben, Fiddler and Kalam: Hairlock is going mad; Sorry and Shadow; a guess that Shadowthrone and Cotillion are Kellanved and Dancer. Fiddler feels something about Sorry and they go out.
- **4.2** Hood's Gate. Oponn trades someone close to Paran for his life. *(R; the "who" is later said by Erikson to be Felisin, per W's Oponn page.)*
- **4.3** Same, then Pale. Shadowthrone lets Paran go to use him; Paran wakes in front of the Bridgeburners and is carried to the barracks.
- **4.4** Tattersail reads again: the Mason of High House Death; the Knight of Darkness against High House Shadow.
- **4.5** Whiskeyjack and Dujek: the Empire means to kill the Bridgeburners; Dujek lets them slip away, and Whiskeyjack says the army will stand behind him.
- **4.6** Bridgeburners and Tattersail: Hairlock hunted by Hounds in the warrens; Sorry as Shadow's tool; someone saved Paran. Tattersail will nurse Paran; Whiskeyjack's squad sets off for Darujhistan. *(The quorl departure is the bible's reading.)*
- **4.7** Tattersail's quarters. The Hound Gear, alone, chases Hairlock there; Paran wounds it with his sword Chance and it flees; both hear a coin spinning. End of Book One.

## Chapter 5 (Darujhistan)

- **5.1** Kruppe's dream: an inn on a hill west of the city, beggars who are his own aspects, and the spinning Coin; he decides to play.
- **5.2** The D'Arle estate. Crokus Younghand robs the room of the sleeping Challice D'Arle.
- **5.3** Rooftops by K'rul's belfry. The Guild assassin Talo Krafar, wounded, fires at Crokus by mistake; Crokus stoops for a dropped coin and the bolt misses; Talo is killed by a hunter from the sky, and two more drop down and chase Crokus. *(W calls the hunters assassin-mages at war with the Guild; that they are Rake's Tiste Andii is the bible's reading and fits 13.3, but the chapter does not name them.)* [?]
- **5.4** Across the city. Crokus runs the thieves' lines, passes his uncle Mammot, and gets to the Phoenix Inn. *(W.)*
- **5.5** Outside the Phoenix. The hunters decide someone is shielding the boy and withdraw. *(W.)*

## Chapter 6 (Darujhistan)

- **6.1** Crone flies from Moon's Spawn to the city on Rake's errand.
- **6.2** Despot's Barbican. Circle Breaker watches Turban Orr meet another councillor and thinks of civil war.
- **6.3** Baruk's house. Baruk reads Circle Breaker's report; Crone asks him to meet Rake; Orr arrives.
- **6.4** Lady Simtal's estate. Rallick Nom overhears Councillor Lim and Simtal on Baruk and a secret cabal.
- **6.5** Baruk's house. Orr wants Baruk behind a neutrality proclamation; Baruk refuses; Crone feels power stir as a coin spins.
- **6.6** The streets. Rallick, hearing the coin, kills Lim instead of Simtal; Ocelot says the Guild is being hunted and Rallick will be bait.
- **6.7** Baruk's house. Rake comes with Dragnipur and wants the heads of the mages who fled Pale; Baruk agrees.
- **6.8** The Phoenix Inn. Crokus, Murillio and Kruppe at cards while Coll sleeps off drink; Rallick comes in.

## Chapter 7 (Darujhistan)

- **7.1** Kruppe's dream. The Elder god K'rul points him at the "awakeners", Lorn and Tool.
- **7.2** Circle Breaker writes a plea to the Eel and burns it.
- **7.3** Simtal's house. Simtal tells Orr off for courting the Malazans for a Fist's title and asks him to have her ex-husband Coll killed.
- **7.4** Murillio arranges two invitations to Simtal's Gedderone's Eve party.
- **7.5** Streets and the Phoenix. Rallick warns Crokus off Orr's estate; Crokus shows Kruppe the coin; Rallick and Murillio resolve to steer the boy straight.
- **7.6** Baruk's house. Baruk is bothered by road crews outside; Kruppe names Crokus as the coin's bearer; Baruk wants him guarded, and Rallick ready to kill him if need be.

## Chapter 8 (the north shore of Lake Azur)

- **8.1** Whiskeyjack's squad and the Moranth: the Moranth hand over munitions and Whiskeyjack drops the Empress's plan for his own. *(The bible cites 8.1 for Green Moranth quorls and the squad's crossing "by boat"; R names neither the clan nor the boat.)* [?]
- **8.2** The warren of Chaos. Hairlock tells Quick Ben of the Hound and Tattersail's wound and says gods are meddling.
- **8.3** A pebbled beach across the lake from the city. Quick Ben's proposal shakes Whiskeyjack and Kalam.

## Chapter 9 (the Rhivi Plain and Pale)

- **9.1** The Rhivi Plain, three days from Pale. Toc finds marines killed by Barghast and a shaman's body, and follows tracks.
- **9.2** An old barrow. Two marines die; Tool rises and saves Lorn; the barrow gives up "a truth"; Toc is there.
- **9.3** Pale. Tattersail wakes; she and Paran talk of the god who saved him and the coin that stopped.
- **9.4** Pale. Lorn meets Dujek, hears the Bridgeburners may be disbanded, and learns Tayschrenn suspects Oponn.
- **9.5** Pale. Tattersail thinks on Pale and on Paran, and learns he is Lorn's man.
- **9.6** Pale. Dinner: Lorn presses Tattersail over the Mouse Quarter; Tattersail lies about the Hound and Toc keeps her lie; afterwards Paran tells her he was sent after Sorry, and they sleep together. *(R's scenes 6 and 7 merged, to keep 9.8 on Crone as the bible has it.)*
- **9.7** Leaving Pale. Tool tells Lorn of the T'lan Imass, the Kron and the coming Gathering.
- **9.8** Over the Rhivi Plain. Crone, flying to Brood, feels change coming.

## Chapter 10 (the Rhivi Plain)

- **10.1** Pale. Toc tells Paran he is a soldier of the Second, not a Claw; Paran will go to Darujhistan and Toc goes with him.
- **10.2** The plain. Tattersail and Bellurdan come out of the warren, hunted; her magic is deadened. *(R's scene 2 split in two, to match the bible's 10.2–10.3.)*
- **10.3** Same. She opens her warren fully and the fire takes them both.
- **10.4** Far off. Tool and Lorn watch the pillar of fire; Tool says the source died but something was born.
- **10.5** Brood's camp. Crone, Brood, Kallor: Brood will shield the Coinbearer and keep Rake and the Empire apart; Kallor is left in charge and mutters.
- **10.6** The burn. Toc and Paran find the charred bodies and bone-footed tracks; Paran blames Tayschrenn and Lorn.
- **10.7** The plain. Hairlock, as a shape-shifter, kills two Great Ravens and nearly kills Crone, who flies to tell Rake.
- **10.8** The plain. Tool tells Lorn an Elder-magic user is tracking them.

## Chapter 11 (Darujhistan)

- **11.1** Kruppe's dream at the beginning of things. Tattersail is reborn through a pregnant Rhivi woman; Pran Chole is told he will see the child's future.
- **11.2** Outside Baruk's house. Kruppe hears a Malazan oath from the road crew (the Bridgeburners laying mines).
- **11.3** The city. Whiskeyjack and Sorry: she may be a Seer, and he despairs of what he is becoming. *(The bible reads this as Whiskeyjack sending Sorry after Kruppe.)* [?]
- **11.4** Mammot's house. Mammot tells Crokus of the Jaghut–Imass wars and a Jaghut barrow near the city.
- **11.5** The streets. Sorry, following Kruppe, crushes the presence waking in her, names herself Cotillion, and means to kill him.
- **11.6** The market. Kruppe steals his lunch by sorcery and goes into the Phoenix.
- **11.7** The Phoenix. Sorry kills a lookout; Crokus finds the body, works out she did it, and she sees he is the Coinbearer. *(R's scenes 7–9 merged, to keep 11.8 as the bible has it.)*
- **11.8** Kalam has failed to reach the local Guild; he and Quick Ben plan to draw Ascendants in.
- **11.9** The Phoenix. Kruppe, Crokus, Coll, Rallick, Murillio: rumours of an alliance with Moon's Spawn and of five black dragons.
- **11.10** By warren. Quick Ben goes into Shadow and hears Hounds.

## Chapter 12 (Darujhistan and Shadow)

- **12.1** Mammot's study. Kruppe reads of a god crippled and chained; Baruk sends for him through Mammot.
- **12.2** Crone tells Baruk of Hairlock on the plain; Baruk tells her of the buried Jaghut Tyrant but not where.
- **12.3** Shadowkeep. Quick Ben trades Hairlock for the lifting of Shadow's order on his life; Shadowthrone knows him too late.
- **12.4** Baruk sets Kruppe to have Murillio, Rallick, Coll and Crokus watch the Gadrobi Hills.
- **12.5** Quick Ben, Kalam and Sorry: she found an assassin at the Phoenix and has another job from Whiskeyjack.
- **12.6** The Phoenix. Ocelot orders Rallick to lead Kalam into a warehouse trap.
- **12.7** The D'Arle house. Crokus starts to break in to return what he stole.

## Chapter 13 (Darujhistan, the night of the rooftops)

- **13.1** The streets. Kalam and Quick Ben follow Rallick toward the warehouse.
- **13.2** The warehouse. Rallick reports; Ocelot sets the ambush.
- **13.3** The rooftops. A dozen Tiste Andii drop from the sky and kill Ocelot's people; Baruk's demon barely gets away.
- **13.4** The rooftops. Kalam and Quick Ben are attacked.
- **13.5** The warehouse. Rallick kills an attacker whose body vanishes; he and Ocelot pull back.
- **13.6** The D'Arle estate. Sorry watches Crokus and kills a guard. *(The killing is in R's commentary and in 17.5, not in R's scene summary.)*
- **13.7** Same. Crokus gives back the jewels, says he will court Challice, and leaves as the alarm goes, seeing Sorry.
- **13.8** The rooftops. Quick Ben gives the demon Pearl its true name and sends it to cover them; Rake comes and Pearl dies.
- **13.9** The streets. Rallick warns Crokus off thieving; Murillio brings Baruk's errand.
- **13.10** The Andii camp. Serrat reports; Rake is pleased that the Claw and the Guild were about to fight.
- **13.11** The squad's quarters. Mallet heals Kalam; the squad works out that Rake wiped out the Guild so the Empire could not use it.
- **13.12** The streets. Sorry overhears Rallick and Murillio and decides to kill Crokus outside the city.
- **13.13** Baruk's house. Rake admits the killings and apologises for not asking; Baruk says Vorcan is a High Mage.
- **13.14** Kruppe's dream. K'rul: Tattersail is growing fast; Dragnipur; Rake; warnings about Imass, Jaghut and the Coinbearer.

## Chapter 14 (toward the Gadrobi Hills)

- **14.1** Tool tells Lorn why he was chosen to free the Tyrant, and of the Tiste Andii's origins.
- **14.2** Kruppe, Crokus, Coll and Murillio set out for the hills on Baruk's errand.
- **14.3** Sorry follows them to kill Crokus, sensing danger ahead.
- **14.4** The barrow. Tool finds it and will open it at dawn; Lorn doubts.
- **14.5** The plain. Paran and Toc find the ravens Hairlock killed; Toc smells an ambush.

## Chapter 15 (the Rhivi Plain and the hills)

- **15.1** Quick Ben spies on Hairlock with Trotts on guard.
- **15.2** Hairlock ambushes Paran and Toc and throws Toc into a warren; Hounds bay. *(Toc's fate is not told in GotM.)*
- **15.3** Quick Ben, through his link to Sorry, tells Cotillion where Hairlock is.
- **15.4** The barrow and Kruppe's camp. Lorn attacks the party, wounds Coll, knocks Murillio out, then lets them be.
- **15.5** Shadowthrone hears Quick Ben's news; Sorry moves on Crokus.
- **15.6** Hairlock, afraid of the Hounds, promises to come back for Paran and tries to escape by warren.
- **15.7** Quick Ben cuts Hairlock's strings.
- **15.8** The Hounds tear Hairlock apart.
- **15.9** Rake arrives and kills two Hounds; Shadowthrone agrees to call Cotillion off Sorry. *(R. The two Hounds' names, Doan and Ganrod, are the bible's and unconfirmed.)* [?]
- **15.10** Paran goes into Dragnipur through a Hound's blood, sees a chained man, leads the Hounds out with Oponn's help, and comes back to the plain. *(R's scene 8 split in two, to match the bible's 15.9 and 15.11.)*
- **15.11** Sorry, free of Cotillion, has no memory; Coll tells Crokus to take her to Mammot.

## Chapter 16 (the hills)

- **16.1** The barrow is opened. Tool says his vows are broken and he will go to find an answer.
- **16.2** Crokus gives Sorry a Daru name: Apsalar.
- **16.3** Kruppe tells Murillio why Oponn's boy needs guarding, and that he knew of Lorn at the barrow.
- **16.4** The plain. Rhivi go for Paran and he gets away; he meets the reborn Tattersail, a small child, who knows him and says they will meet again.
- **16.5** The road. Paran, now his own man, meets the wounded Coll; they go into the city together, trading stories.

## Chapter 17 (Darujhistan)

- **17.1** The Phoenix. The Eel's agent tells Rallick that Orr, for Simtal, has hired Ocelot to kill Coll.
- **17.2** Rake to Baruk: a fight is coming and he will stop Laseen without wrecking the city; Mammot is shown in a trance, trapped in the barrow, a High Priest of D'rek.
- **17.3** Outside the Phoenix. Circle Breaker signals Meese and goes to his last contact expecting to die.
- **17.4** Meese goes into Mammot's house.
- **17.5** Mammot's house. Meese warns Crokus and Apsalar that D'Arle wants them for the guard Apsalar killed.
- **17.6** The Phoenix. Murillio gets a message from the Eel through Circle Breaker.
- **17.7** K'rul's belfry. Rallick rubs otataral dust into his skin and waits for Ocelot.

## Chapter 18 (Darujhistan)

- **18.1** Whiskeyjack's squad argue over the Guild; Quick Ben cannot find Sorry; the squad tells Whiskeyjack he keeps them at arm's length.
- **18.2** The Worry Gate. Paran brings Coll in, badly hurt; a guard knows Coll. *(Gate named in the bible; R says "city gate".)*
- **18.3** K'rul's belfry. Rallick climbs, kills Ocelot, and is stabbed deep in the chest. *(R's scenes 3 and 5 merged.)*
- **18.4** The gate. Paran sees movement on the tower; the guard brings a wagon for Coll.
- **18.5** Outside Mammot's house. Serrat comes to after a blow from behind and slips into her warren.
- **18.6** The Phoenix. Meese and Irilta on Paran and Coll, and the Eel's order to keep Crokus and Apsalar in; Paran weighs his sword; Kalam comes in and is sent for Mallet. *(R's scenes 8–10 merged.)*
- **18.7** The Phoenix. Mallet heals Coll; Paran tells Whiskeyjack of the betrayal and Tattersail's death; Whiskeyjack raises Dujek on the bone device: the Empire means to break up his army, the Moranth are pulling back, and the Pannion Seer is the threat.
- **18.8** The barrow. Tool and Lorn hunt the Finnest that holds the Tyrant's power; her sword will muffle it; they leave as the Jaghut stirs.

## Chapter 19 (Darujhistan)

- **19.1** The Phoenix roof. Crokus and Apsalar slip out to find Challice; Serrat attacks from hiding and is thrown off by something unseen. *(R's scenes 1–3 merged.)*
- **19.2** Rallick's wound has closed and the otataral dust has gone into his skin; Murillio goes to face the Eel, suspecting Kruppe.
- **19.3** Kruppe and Baruk: Baruk wants the Eel as an ally; Kruppe will pass it on.
- **19.4** Whiskeyjack and Paran: Paran guesses the city is the prize; Whiskeyjack says the Pannion Seer matters more. *(The bible says Whiskeyjack and Dujek; R has Whiskeyjack only.)*
- **19.5** Lorn leaves Tool and makes for the city, her wound healing because of the otataral sword. *(The bible's "Tool offers to stay ten days" is not in R.)* [?]
- **19.6** K'rul's belfry. Crokus and Apsalar hide there, find Ocelot's body, and see winged shapes leave Moon's Spawn.

## Chapter 20 (the Fête, the day)

- **20.1** Murillio worries over Rallick's blood loss; Kruppe hands out masks and makes Murillio forget that he is the Eel.
- **20.2** Baruk cannot keep Rake from the party; Mammot wakes from the barrow trance; Rake leaves.
- **20.3** The Worry Gate. Lorn enters the city, healing slower than she hoped; Circle Breaker knows her from the Eel's description and swaps his shift to be at the party. *(R's scenes 3–4 merged.)*
- **20.4** Quip's Bar. The squad expected Lorn; Whiskeyjack tells her of the mines, the Guild and Sorry's loss; she takes command; he says the squad will guard the party; she will be back in two hours. *(R's scenes 5–6 merged.)*
- **20.5** Quick Ben, Trotts and Whiskeyjack: Kalam is restless, Trotts reports success, Lorn did not bring what Paran expected.
- **20.6** K'rul's tower and after. Apsalar admits her inner war; Serrat is caught and told, on a Prince's behalf, to warn Rake off Crokus. *(R's scenes 8–9 merged.)*
- **20.7** The barrow. Raest wakes and goes after the Finnest.
- **20.8** Above the hills. Crone sees five dragons; Raest strikes and the ground shakes; Silanah and four Soletaken fight him. *(R's scenes 11–12 merged.)*

## Chapter 21 (the Fête, the night)

- **21.1** A garden (Simtal's). Lorn plants the Finnest and resolves to kill Crokus and take the Coin.
- **21.2** Kruppe on the way: someone is guarding Crokus; Circle Breaker may retire after tonight.
- **21.3** Crokus and Apsalar head to the party.
- **21.4** Simtal's party. Whiskeyjack's squad are hired as guards; Quick Ben says the Jaghut is free and winning in the hills.
