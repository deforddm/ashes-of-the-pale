# Ashes of the Pale: forward-canon check at v3.15.3

Wed Oct 7, 2026, 2:58 PM PDT. A read-only check of the game's text and of the first audit (`audit-v3.15.3.md`) against the books that come after *Gardens of the Moon*, because the game may be carried through the whole series. Nothing in the game was changed. Line numbers are for v3.15.3; v3.15.4 (the End Turn guard) changes no story text.

**How it was checked.** Four separate readers each took a slice: the Host and the plain, Darujhistan and Rake, lore and names, and the endings. Each read the game's lines and checked them against the Malazan Wiki, the Reactor re-read of every book, and a fan worksheet that cites the novels by page. I then re-read the quoted game lines and re-fetched four of the canon points myself (Silverfox's age, Moon's Spawn, the Hound named Pallick, the "Chain" card). **Nobody had the novels' text in front of them.** Every item says how sure it is; anything marked *memory* was not checked at all. The wiki refused about half of all requests, so some pages were never reached (listed at the end).

Books are abbreviated: GotM (*Gardens of the Moon*), DG (*Deadhouse Gates*), MoI (*Memories of Ice*), HoC (*House of Chains*), MT (*Midnight Tides*), BH (*The Bonehunters*), TtH (*Toll the Hounds*), DoD (*Dust of Dreams*), OST (*Orb Sceptre Throne*), RotCG (*Return of the Crimson Guard*).

### Medium confidence: check the novel before changing

- **Seasons.** 46:2267 "all winter", :2302 "the whole first winter", :2309 "In the spring", :2278 "It took a year". The Fete is the first night of spring and Coral falls the same year; there is no winter camp. Use "all that season", "that first campaign".
- **Where the Host is.** 46:263 "the Host on the road… walking toward a conversation"; 46:408 Dujek "a thousand leagues off". The Host sits outside Pale for about two months before the parley. "the Host, outside Pale… waiting on a conversation".
- **The empire road.** 46:2178 "Weeks on the road to Genabaris, round the lake"; also :2136, :1610, :272. Genabaris is far to the north-west, past Pale. Either go by water like Kalam's trader, or "north-west, wide of Pale".
- **Otataral and the Elder warrens.** 16b_tricks:33, :36 mark A Courtesy of Darkness (Kurald Galain) and Omtose Rime (Omtose Phellack) "Dead in otataral". Otataral does not quell Elder magic. Exempt the two tricks. This lets them work beside Lorn's sword, the one rules change in this list.
- **Otataral's reach.** 44:514 Tuft feels nothing "three hundred paces" from Lorn. The effect sits on whoever holds or is dusted with it. "I can feel it from here, like a tooth." 16b_tricks:29: it is mined in Seven Cities' Otataral hills, not "where nothing magical has lived since the old wars".
- **Nathilog.** 44:651 "Two years… Chub gave me that one at Nathilog" (also 14_data:5, :30; 43:1325). Nathilog fell early in the campaign; two years ago the Second was at Pale. Drop the number or make it six.
- **The T'lan Imass.** 44:210 "The Empire has an army of them"; glossary 35:100 "They fight beside the Empire." Under Laseen they no longer do. "had"; "fought beside the old Emperor".
- **Lorn and Tool.** 44:775, :779, :1389 send Tool to the city with her. He does not go. The first audit called this optional; it is a real fix.
- **The Azath's yard.** 46:734 Kruppe: "a yard full of… tenants". It has taken one. "a yard with room for".
- **Moon's Spawn before the Fete.** The game hangs it "over the lake" (42:198, :817; 45:49). The wiki puts it south of the city, perhaps over the Dwelling Plain. Check the novel and move it if so; it also changes the Ch3 and Ch6 skies in the art.

## 2. Changes to the first audit

- **§1.3, Lorn's alley.** Harmonising cloak colour and the number of cuts is not enough; both versions give Blues one sword. Apply fix 11 either way.
- **§1.4 #53** is no longer optional (above).
- **§1.5, Moon's Spawn, "pick one sky".** Do not settle on west. West at first light, south after, and check its station before the Fete.
- **§1.5, the Chains card** was "record as deliberate". It collides with the House of Chains; see §4.
- **§1.5, the Mouse Quarter.** "The Claw" → "the cadre mages" holds up. Safest wording: "the new mage cadre and the soldiers with them".
- **§1.5, the glossary.** Onearm's Host: "what is left of the Second, Fifth and Sixth".
- **§1.6** lists the warrens as clean. The otataral rule above is the exception.
- **§6 call 1.** A fan worksheet citing the novel's glossary spells the road "Jammit's Worry"; the map says "Jatem's". **Call 5** is cured by direction alone: the Gadrobi District is the harbour district in the west.
- **Rallick's tiger mask** is supported. Rake wore a black dragon mask with silver tracing and Mammot a tusked Jaghut mask; useful for the Fete art.
- **Two readers disagreed and here is the ruling.** One wanted Paran to leave Lorn's sword behind; the epilogue of GotM has him wearing it, so the game stands and the sword becomes a lock (§3). One wanted the leg left alone; three sources say the wording is the problem, so fix 4 stands.

Checked and safe against the later books: the Darujhistan map fixes, every vision fix, Rake's departure from the belfry, the five dragons, the morning ship, Ocelot's death before the Fete, Hedge alive, the Black Moranth, Dujek naming the Pannion Seer, Lorn buried on the north shore of the lake.

## 3. Keep open: what the game must never pin down

These are right or vague today, and later books depend on them.

1. **The outlawing.** Characters may believe it. The narrator never vouches for it, and Dujek and Whiskeyjack never get a private thought that treats it as real. Dujek's "I haven't decided yet what I'm going to lie about" (46:392) already reads true. The grey cloak's pardon stands as one field agent's belief.
2. **Guilt at Pale.** Varrow's journal records an order and its timing; Tuft and the deserters speak belief. Keep it that way. MoI recasts the enfilade (Nightchill moved first; the tunnels were thought the safe place). Never narrate Tayschrenn killing the cadre.
3. **The cadre badge as the High Mage's window** is Quick Ben's claim. Never show who is on the other end. Later books make Tayschrenn withdrawn, and present in the Host.
4. **The child.** She holds three souls (Tattersail, Nightchill, Bellurdan). The game names one and never says "only". The Rhivi's "east" is a first leg; they are with Brood near Pale within two months. Tuft's "where he can't see" stays her hope.
5. **Toc.** "On neither roll" is exactly right. Never say whose tracks Ellis found or that he was walking. Nobody learns of Morn. Ellis's hunt must not succeed.
6. **The Azath.** Raest becomes the House's walking guardian; Rallick and Vorcan stay inside until TtH. Never open the door, show the inside, say who lies under the fresh mound, or call Raest dead. Never name Vorcan.
7. **Whiskeyjack's leg** stays bad. Nobody heals it.
8. **Lorn's sword.** Paran carries it north but never uses or loses it; by BH it lies buried at Coll's estate.
9. **Simtal.** The shut door is enough. Keep Coll's broken-off "and my wife —"; never say they were childless.
10. **The mines** stay asleep under the crossing. The Claw's order to fire them stays unsigned.
11. **Tuft and the powers.** Noticed by Shadow, never given a House title or card. The "somebody in the house" of Kurald Galain stays Rake or his people, never Mother Dark. Her two Elder tricks are a gift and a frost-mark, never a warren she can open. The blank card stays blank; the Herald and the Magi stay faceless.
12. **The Fourth** is never called Bridgeburners (Paran has exactly thirty-eight) and is always "Seventh Company, Onearm's Host" (Fiddler leads a Fourth Squad in the Fourteenth Army later).
13. **Names the game never uses, and should not:** Kellanved, Dancer, Ammanas, Laseen, Surly, Topper, the Talon, the Eel, the T'orrud Cabal. No Burn's Sleep year: dates stay relative ("nine years before Pale").
14. **Who lives.** No Hound dies at the Fourth's hands. Challice's rescuer stays unseen. Murillio, Coll, Baruk, Derudan, Kruppe, Crone and Rake all walk out of the game alive and unexplained. Blues does not die.
15. **The chained man in vision 5** stays nameless.
16. **Rumjugs and Sweetlard** stay a pin-up nobody can explain; they join the Bonehunters five books on.

## 5. If the game goes on

**The roads meet again.** In canon the Host is back at Pale within weeks, and Brood's army, with the Rhivi and the child, meets it there about two months after the Fete. The outlaw road, Brisk going to Tav, Ohl's hospital tent, Kettle with the Black Moranth, and Tuft and Ohl going east all end up in one camp. Only the empire road, the city road and Ellis truly part. That camp is the natural place to open a sequel.

| Book | When and where | Which road reaches it | Hard limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| MoI | From two months after the Fete: Pale, the parley, Capustan, Coral | Outlaw, and everyone who went north or east | Not among Paran's thirty-eight; not in Coral's keep. Whiskeyjack, Hedge and Twist die at Coral; six Bridgeburners survive. The two marines guarding Silverfox are killed. The Host falls from about 10,000 to about 3,000. |
| DG | Same year, Seven Cities | Empire, if its ship calls there | The Chain of Dogs and Aren leave almost no survivors. Kalam and Fiddler are there. |
| HoC | The year after | Outlaw (the Host's remnant sails to Seven Cities); empire (the Fourteenth Army) | The Fourteenth's marine squads are named almost man for man. |
| BH | The year after that | Outlaw: Paran takes command of the Host. City: Paran passes through to dig up Lorn's sword | Plague; Dujek dies. |
| TtH, OST | Some years on, Darujhistan | City | K'rul's Bar and the retired Bridgeburners; a Guild contract on Malazans; Rake dies in the streets; the Legate. Kettle's Moranth tie and the gas matter at last. |
| RotCG | After BH, Quon Tali | Empire: the best "home" for that road | Units are loosely listed, so there is room. |
| DoD, *The Crippled God* | Kolanse | Outlaw survivors under Paran | The Bonehunters' squads end nearly all named. |
| MT, *Reaper's Gale*, *Stonewielder*, *Blood and Bone* | | Off the map | |

Rich roads: outlaw first, then city, then empire. Off the map: Tuft and Ohl east after Coral, Ellis after Toc, Kettle with the Moranth until OST.

**What a sequel inherits from the player.** None of these contradict canon as written, so long as the rules in §3 hold: squadmates dead in Chapter 6; a squad that reported to the Claw; a pardon (worthless once the Host is reinstated after Coral); the unsigned order to fire the mines; a mage marked by Shadow or by Rake's people; a scout who went into the grey and came back; Sethand's vow of silence about the child, which explains why no canon character ever hears of her from the Fourth. The gods who answer a wipe will need replacing by book: Fener falls in DG and Hood dies in TtH.
