# Ashes of the Pale v3.18.0: Doctor Brack's stall

Shipped Thu Oct 8, 2026. David asked for a merchant stall at the Worry Gate: "a traveling dealer of wonders and snake oils etc. who has decided to set up shop while his entry is delayed. have fun with it."

## Who
**Doctor Ottavio Brack**, *Purveyor of Wonders, Remedies & the Occasional Lesser Miracle*, lately of Seven Cities, Unta, Pale and Elsewhere. No refunds in this life.
- **Why he's stuck:** he declared his goods to Pennick as "remedies, wonders, and lesser miracles". Pennick's ledger has no column for miracles, so Pennick has written to the Council for a ruling, and the Council does not read it.
- **Where:** nine days in the inbound queue at the Worry Gate (Ch3 area `worry_gate`, tile 9,9), between an onion cart and a Gadrobi family arguing about a goat.
- **His stall:** a figure sprite `brack` with a striped awning the colour of old soup, a counter of bottles catching the lamplight, a coat of too many colours, a hat and a waxed moustache.

## v3.18.1 (Thu Oct 8, evening)
- **Rumjugs & Sweetlard:** the top banner on the crate lid moved down 40 (its arc centre went from y 980 to 1020 in `src/19b_pinup.js`). The R and the D are no longer cut off by the arch.
- **Fire** (`enterFire`, 31_battle.js). Any unit that steps into a burning tile, or is dragged or driven through one, takes 1d4 and makes a save.
  - This covers walking, enemy moves, the hook, Shield Drive, enemy shoves, Rope Swing, Shadow Step and the follow-through.
  - The squad's save is d20 + the better of Might and Guile (+2 if Steady) against 12 + the difficulty. An enemy's is d20 + half its to-hit against 12. A natural 1 always fails and a natural 20 always succeeds.
  - On a fail the unit is on fire for 2 turns: 1d4 at the start of each. It shows as "on fire (n turns)" on the unit bar, with an orange glow.
  - Rally, Denul Wash and the Rhivi Spirit-Song put it out. Standing in fire at the start of a turn still costs 1d6, as before.
  - The enemy shove no longer double-charges for fire.
- New `tools/fire_test.mjs` (4 checks). It freezes the fight while the drive is tested.
