# Book Two: Deadhouse Gates — the book brief (Chapters 2–7)

The standing work order for every remaining chapter of Book Two. Each chapter is built by a **writer** (content, tests) and an **artist** (drawings) working at the same time, then reviewed and published as its own version before the next chapter starts. Read the shared rules, then your chapter's section.

## Shared rules (writers)

Read first, in order: `docs/dg-canon.md` (the sourced canon sheet and its hard limits), `docs/dg-continuity.md` (what earlier Book Two chapters set up; **honour every flag and invention there**), `docs/dg-ch1-brief.md` (the Chapter 1 work order: the schema, the squad, the Chain, ids, styles, tests; everything it says still holds unless this brief changes it), `src/47_book2.js`, the previous Book Two chapter file(s) (`src/48_b2_ch1.js` …) for the established voice of *this* book, and `src/40_chapter1.js`/`41_chapter2.js` for Book One's voice. Match the voice: short paragraphs, dry soldier humour, grimdark restraint (the owner: "not a game for kids… not looking for anything lurid"), no exclamation marks in narration, second-person sergeant with no stated gender, `{sgt}`, italics with `*asterisks*`.

- **Your file:** `src/NN_b2_chK.js` exporting `B2CHK` (Ch2 = `49_b2_ch2.js`/`B2CH2`, Ch3 = `50_b2_ch3.js`/`B2CH3`, Ch4 = `51`, Ch5 = `52`, Ch6 = `53`, Ch7 = `54`). Register it in `src/90_resume_boot.js` beside `B2CH1`: `if (typeof B2CHK !== 'undefined') registerChapter(K, B2CHK, 'dg');`. `number:'Two'` etc.
- **Ids** all carry `b2_` (areas, nodes, fights, foes, gear, scenes, tricks, flags `S.f.b2_*`). Don't reuse a Chapter 1 id for something new.
- **Canon.** Before writing, do a focused canon re-check of your chapter's stretch of *Deadhouse Gates* (WebSearch/WebFetch: the Malazan Wiki at malazan.fandom.com, which often refuses requests, and the Reactor/Tor.com Malazan re-read of DG). Add what you confirm or correct to `docs/dg-canon.md` under a dated "Chapter K re-check" heading, with sources. Canon characters may now appear when the canon sheet places them on the Chain at that point (Lull, List, Nil, Nether, Mincer, Bungle, Cuttle, Chenned, Sulmar, the Nobles' Council, Gesler, Stormy, Truth…), in their own book voices, without the squad ever taking a canon deed from them, saving anyone canon kills, or killing anyone canon spares. If unsure of a detail, keep it vague rather than invent a contradiction. Never meet Felisin, Heboric, Baudin, Kalam, Fiddler, Crokus, Apsalar, Mappo, Icarium or the Path of Hands.
- **Size.** About the size of Chapter 1: 60–80 nodes, three or four areas (16×12), three or four fights, each main fight with a `stage2`. A chapter adds about 500–700 experience at the engine's 0.6 pay (the squad is level 3 after Ch1; aim for roughly level 4 after Ch2, 5 after Ch3, 6 after Ch4, 6–7 after Ch5, 7 after Ch6, 8 by the end). Tune with `tools/balance.mjs` (a scratch `--patch` file; don't change the tool) so the smart player wins every fight on Soldier and Bridgeburner stays hard.
- **Key choice** each chapter: `S.f.b2_cK_key` (Ch1's is `b2_key`), passed to `chapterEnd(K, key)`, two or three values, each with an `end` entry, and real consequences later. Plant, then pay off.
- **The Chain.** Every chapter trades water against the column (`chain()`, `chainHTML()`, `dgRest()`); at least one scene shows the march widget. The column shrinks in the bad days as well as growing.
- **Squad arcs** to carry forward (pay off across the book; don't resolve early unless your section says so): the sergeant and the column ("you own these"; Ressan's daughters); Haddat and her brother Ilhar in the rebellion; Sathi, the Weasel Clan's ways and the crows; Tangle's satchel that is never refilled; Marrow's fouled warren and her knotted string; the Red Blade recruit from Ch2 on.
- **Squad lines** branch by roller on this squad's ids with a `_` default; `edges` favour the right squadmate.
- **Tricks.** Book Two adds lore tricks the way Book One did (`src/16b_tricks.js`, `src/31b_tricks_in_battle.js`): about one per chapter, behind an optional ✦ hard check (DC 15–17), defined in your chapter file with `Object.assign(TRICKS, {b2_…})` and an ability in `AB` if it has a battle half. Ideas: Wickan spirit-craft (Ch2/3), sapper's lore (Ch2), tribal cant (Haddat), the Red Blade oath, Jaghut cold from the tower (Ch5, elder), Khundryl war-paint (Ch6).
- **No Deck reading** in Book Two (no `card`) unless your section asks for one.
- **Art.** Use only the sprite kinds, tile styles/characters and backdrop names your section lists (the artist is drawing them now in `src/NNb_b2_chK_art.js`), plus everything already registered (`src/47b_book2_art.js` and earlier chapter art files). The artist's file starts as placeholders, which is fine for tests.
- **Engine changes** go in `src/47_book2.js` (Book Two only, guarded by `S.book === 'dg'`), never into Book One files unless a bug blocks you; report any such edit exactly.
- **Continuity.** When done, append your chapter's section to `docs/dg-continuity.md`: the key values and what each changes, every flag later chapters should read (with values), named inventions (people, places, objects), and anything left open on purpose.
- **Tests (all must pass):** `python3 build.py`; `node --check` on your files; `node tools/lint.mjs` 0 errors; `node tools/play.mjs 3 --book=dg` 3/3 (the bot plays Book Two from Chapter 1 through to your chapter's end screen; if it can't reach your chapter in time, add/extend a `--from=K` start for Book Two, built with `newStateDG` and the earlier chapters' typical flags, and run both); `node tools/play.mjs 2` (Book One) 2/2; then `node tools/run_all.mjs` PASS. Don't commit or push; don't touch `sw.js` or `src/10_preamble.js`.
- **Report** (under 500 words): contents (areas, nodes, fights and xp, the key and its consequences), flags, file size, test numbers, edits outside your files, canon points you were unsure of.

## Chapter 2 — "The Odhan" (v4.1.0)

**Spine** (dg-canon §1): months pass on the march (Sekala is about 3 months and 160 leagues out of Hissar). Coltaine leaves the refugees at **Dryj Spring** and rides for water; outnumbered seven to one, the Wickans destroy a Tithansi force in the Odhan. **Sekala Crossing**: Captain Mincer's sappers pave the ford under the water and pack it with 55 cussers; the sapper **Cuttle** blows it once the Malazans are over; Reloe on the east bank; Guran heavy infantry, Tithansi lancers, Semk warriors and sorcerers on the west; the Sialk Marines and the Red Blades each lose about half. Afterwards **Sormo, Nil and Nether** set the land's spirits on the Semk god, which tears it apart (the squad may witness it from a distance; it must stay the warlocks' deed).

**The squad's story:** the long march montage and the first real thirst; the Chain widget on the road. At Dryj Spring the key choice (`b2_c2_key`): **`spring`** (stay and guard the refugees at the spring: a fight at the spring, column up) or **`water`** (ride with the Wickans for water: Sathi and Hackle shine, water up, the column at the spring suffers in the squad's absence). At Sekala, Tangle works the ford with Mincer's sappers (never the one who blows it: that is Cuttle). The fight on the west bank among the Guran and Semk.

**New:** (1) **Fouled warrens** become a mechanic: Book Two spells that cost strain (Marrow's, and enemy casters') can **sputter** for half effect with a short "the warren gives back mud" line; chance 10% in Ch2, rising 5% a chapter to 35% by Ch7, and Marrow ties a knot only for a clean one (track `S.f.b2_knots` / `b2_sputters` for her string). Wickan spirit-craft and elder tricks never sputter. Implement in `src/47_book2.js`. (2) **The Red Blade recruit**, offered after Sekala when half the Red Blades are dead: a Seven Cities loyalist in the Red Blades, distrusted by the Malazans and hated by the rebels. Name them yourself (check the name against the canon for clashes: not Baria, Mesker, Lostara, Tene, nor anything close), add their TPL entry, VERB, STATUP, PICKS[3], PATRONS entry, and the recruit's joining scene (`recruit('b2_redblade')`-style id: use id **`redblade`**). One recruit at a time. They leave in Ch7 when the Red Blades are arrested in Aren (canon), so plant their loyalty to the unit.

**Haddat:** first word of Ilhar: a captured green-cloth says a man of the water-sellers' quarter rides with Reloe's Tithansi (`b2_ilharHeard`).

**Art (artist, `src/49b_b2_ch2_art.js`):** figures `redblade` (recruit + generic Red Blade: Seven Cities loyalist, red-enamelled scale or red-dyed leather, curved blade), `guran` (Guran heavy infantry, rebel city legion, armoured), `mincer` (Captain Mincer, a sapper captain: older, burn-scarred, pockets), `sapper` (generic Malazan sapper), `nil` (young Wickan warlock, a boy), `nether` (young Wickan warlock, a girl), `tithansi_rider` (a Tithansi on foot with a horse-bow). Portrait `redblade`. Ground: style `river` (a broad shallow river and its banks by day: `.` bank mud, `,` reeds, `~` deep water (blocks), `-` shallow ford water (walkable, wet), `p` paved ford stones (walkable), `#` cut bank (blocks), `r` rock, `s` sand, `W` wagon, `=` tent). Extend `odhan`/`odhan_night` with `o` (spring pool, blocks) and `t` (tamarisk). Backdrops `b2_dryj_spring` (a muddy spring crowded with people and cattle, heat), `b2_sekala` (the ford at dawn with two armies on the banks), `b2_spirits` (night: the land's spirits rising out of the earth to tear at something huge and painted; suggest, from far off).

## Chapter 3 — "The Ridge" (v4.2.0)

**Spine:** the **night raid** about 9 days before the P'atha River: Captain **Lull**'s party, with Duiker and marines, goes in through a tunnel raised by the Wickan warlocks and kills the Tithansi warleader, possessed by what is left of the Semk god; Claw weapons turn up in the bodies; the tunnel's water ends the army's thirst. **Gelor Ridge** at the P'atha: Reloe's ramp funnels the Malazans uphill (4 tribes, 6 city legions in three groups, Tithansi cavalry hidden behind the Malazan lines); Nil and Nether sacrifice a mare to power a charge by about 5,000 **Foolish Dog** heavy cavalry; the sappers ambush from the ramp's banks; the **Weasel Clan uses refugees as bait** to trap the Tithansi (hundreds of refugees die; the Tithansi are all but wiped out). Lull loses an eye and his nose. Reloe never commands again; **Korbolo Dom** takes the rebel army (name him, offstage).

**The squad's story:** a second tunnel or a second party beside Lull's (never his kill; the squad might find the Claw weapons, or the water). At Gelor the Weasels' bait is Sathi's clan's way and the squad's stretch of the column is in it. Key `b2_c3_key`: **`bait`** (herd your refugees into the trap as ordered: column down hard, the line holds, the Weasels honour Sathi) or **`pull`** (pull your people out of the bait at a cost to the line: a harder fight, column kept, Sathi shamed before her clan). **Art (`src/50b_b2_ch3_art.js`):** figures `lull` (Captain Lull of the Sialk Marines: practical, dry, a marine captain; before his wound), `foolish_dog` (Foolish Dog Clan heavy rider on foot, scale and lance), `weasel` (a Weasel Clan warrior, teeth filed after Sekala), `warleader` (the possessed Tithansi warleader: painted, wrong, sickly green), `claw` already exists from Book One (check), `guran_officer`.
