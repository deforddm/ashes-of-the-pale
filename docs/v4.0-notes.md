# v4.0.0 notes: the shelf and Book Two, Chapter One

Sun Oct 11, 2026. Book Two (*Deadhouse Gates*) begins as its own campaign; Book One plays exactly as before.

## The shelf (engine)

- **Books.** `BOOKS` and `BOOKSNAP` in `src/14_data.js`. Each save carries `S.book` (`'gotm'` or `'dg'`; old saves are `'gotm'`). `registerChapter(n, CH, book)` keeps another book's chapters aside; `setBook(b)` (`src/15_state.js`, called by `migrate`) swaps `CHAPTERS`, `CHEND`, `CHTEASE` and `TPL.sgt` to the save's book. `BOOK` is the book in play.
- **Shared by id.** Areas, nodes, fights, foes, gear and scenes are global, so every Book Two id carries `b2_` (flags `S.f.b2_*`).
- **Title.** The new-sergeant form has a Book picker (Book One / Book Two) and, for Book Two, an optional import of a finished Book One sergeant. Roster rows say which book.
- **Kept out of Book Two:** Book One's painted chapter plates and end vistas (`artPlate`, `ART_ENDVISTA`), its journal lines, end-screen lines, maps and route map, and the Maud rule for cussers. Book Two brings its own journal (`CH.journal`), end lines (`CH.extras`) and glossary (`GLOSSX.dg`).
- **Hooks for later books' art:** `FIGX` (figures, `18`), `TILEX` (ground, `25`), `SCENEX` (backdrops, `23`); `POR` takes portraits as before. Ambience names for Book Two's places are in `13`.
- **Healer text** names whoever heals (`HEALER()`: Ohl or Marrow). Hackle is not called a stranger in the battle log.
- **Not done yet:** book packs that load on demand. Book Two ships inside `index.html` for now (about 290 KB with its art); split it out when the book grows.

## Book Two systems (`src/47_book2.js`)

- Squad `['sgt','haddat','sathi','tangle','marrow']`; their TPL, talents (generic keys reused from Book One), stat growth and gods (`PATRONS`: the Wickan spirits, Oponn, Hood, Soliel; Fener for the sergeant).
- **Hackle**: an ally beside Sathi in every fight (`battleDef` wrapper; `noDog:true` opts a fight out), scaled by level, and he cancels an enemy ambush.
- **The Chain**: `S.chain = {water, column}`; `chain(dw, dc, why)`, `chainHTML(road)`, `dgRest()`. Water 0 means every squadmate starts a fight 3 down. The map header shows both numbers.
- **Import**: `newStateDG(name, importId)` stores `S.imported = {from, road, lvl, follow, mates, dead, loy}`; `dgOldHands()` lists empire-road survivors for Chapter 7.

## Chapter One, Hissar Burns (`src/48_b2_ch1.js`)

Four areas (`b2_quarter`, `b2_gate`, `b2_camp`, `b2_basin`), 73 nodes, fights `b2_street`, `b2_cistern` (optional), `b2_gate`, `b2_batrol`. Key `S.f.b2_key`: `daughters` (−3 water, Ressan's daughters Imra and Dessi in the column, `b2_ressanOut`) or `carts` (+4 water; the girls' fate left open). Flags for later chapters: `b2_ressanOut`, `b2_girlsOut`, `b2_dipper`, `b2_ilharDoor` (`left`/`taken`), `b2_listSeen`, `b2_sathiCrows`, `b2_sormo`, `b2_yedda`, `b2_cistern` (`talked`/`blown`/`fought`), `b2_mother` (`gave`/`refused`), `b2_relSeen`, `b2_duikerTold`, `b2_bult`, `b2_duiker`, `b2_reloe`.

Inventions to keep consistent: Duiker came to Hissar to write the history of a quiet province; Sormo's apprentices are shown but not named; Mallick Rel is escorted out of the city by Wickans and comes to no harm; Reloe stays offstage at Bat'rol.

Art (`src/47b_book2_art.js`): the four squadmates and Hackle, 17 Book Two figures, four portraits, three ground styles (`hissar`, `odhan`, `odhan_night`) and six backdrops.
