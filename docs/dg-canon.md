# Deadhouse Gates: canon sheet for Book Two

Sun Oct 11, 2026, 4:35 PM PDT. Built for Book Two of Ashes of the Pale, a standalone campaign with an original squad in Coltaine's Seventh Army on the Chain of Dogs. One researcher read the Malazan Wiki and the Reactor re-read of *Deadhouse Gates* (chapters 12 to 23). Nobody had the novel's text. Marks: **[H]** two sources agree, **[1]** one source, **[M]** memory, not checked.

Name fixes: it is **Aren Way** (a raised road about 3 leagues into Aren), not "Aren's Way". **Gelor Ridge comes between Sekala and the Vathar**, and Sanimon comes after the Vathar. Wikipedia's plot summary gets Coltaine's death wrong (it has Squint shooting Reloe); the wiki and the re-read agree Squint shot Coltaine.

## 1. The Chain, in order

The wiki's list: Hissar, Bat'rol, Dryj Spring, Sekala Crossing, Gelor Ridge, Vathar Crossing, Nenoth (the Dry March), Sanimon, Tathimon, Dojal Spring, Balahn, the Ramp, the Barrow [1; everything checkable matches the order].

- **Hissar.** Rebels take the city; Sialk falls too. The Wickans ride round the first attackers, ambush them, and pull back with many citizens before rebel reinforcements arrive. [H]
- **Bat'rol** (Vin'til Basin, south-west of Hissar and Sialk). First real battle. Kamist Reloe's eager, green Odhan army is smashed and regroups at Meila Oasis. The column: Seventh regulars, loyal Hissari auxiliaries and Wickans, escorting over 40,000 refugees. [H]
- **Dryj Spring.** Coltaine leaves the refugees at the spring and goes for water; outnumbered seven to one, the Wickans destroy a Tithansi force in the Odhan. [1]
- **Sekala Crossing**, about 3 months and 160 leagues out of Hissar. Captain Mincer's sappers pave the ford under the water and pack it with 55 cussers; Cuttle blows it once the Malazans are over. Reloe holds the east bank; Guran heavy infantry, Tithansi lancers and Semk warriors and sorcerers hold the west. The Sialk Marines and the Red Blades each lose about half. Afterwards Sormo, Nil and Nether set the land's spirits on the Semk god, which tears it apart. [H]
- **Night raid**, about 9 days before the P'atha River. Captain Lull's party, with Duiker and marines, goes in through a tunnel raised by the warlocks and kills the Tithansi warleader, possessed by what is left of the Semk god. Claw weapons turn up in the bodies. The tunnel's water ends the army's thirst. [H]
- **Gelor Ridge** (at the P'atha River). Reloe's ramp funnels the Malazans uphill: 4 tribes and 6 city legions in three groups, with Tithansi cavalry hidden behind the Malazan lines. Nil and Nether sacrifice a mare to power a charge by about 5,000 Foolish Dog heavy cavalry; the sappers ambush from the ramp's banks; the Weasel Clan uses refugees as bait to trap the Tithansi (hundreds of refugees die; the Tithansi are all but wiped out). Lull loses an eye and his nose. Reloe never commands again; Korbolo Dom takes the whole rebel army. [H]
- **Vathar Crossing**, "the Day of Pure Blood". Dom offers safe passage; Coltaine refuses; the nobles make a secret deal with Dom. Dom's archers and pikemen on floating bridges slaughter the refugees: about 20,000 refugees, about 700 soldiers and Sormo E'nath die; the Foolish Dog Clan falls below 500. The *Silanda* is at the ford and takes the badly wounded to Aren. Coltaine breaks his hand punching Gesler. Mincer is broken to the ranks; Bungle is made captain. [H]
- **Vathar Forest.** Old T'lan Imass and Jaghut battlefields, Jaghut tombs and a Jaghut tower. [1]
- **Nenoth Odhan, the Dry March.** Bhilard and Tregyn raid without pause; Dom's army grows to about five times Coltaine's; the last cattle die. Karpolan Demesand of the Trygalle Trade Guild arrives by carriage with food and water (Dujek sent him, the Cabal paid) and a smoky glass bottle for Coltaine, which Coltaine gives to Duiker. [H]
- **Sanimon.** Tens of thousands of Khundryl, seemingly Dom's, turn on him and break the Bhilard, Can'eld, Semk, Tithan and Tregyn. Their war chief names the Wickans the strongest tribe. [H]
- **The running retreat** (Tathimon, Dojal Spring). Dom attacks for more than 40 hours; the Chain is down to about 5,000 soldiers. On the Geleen Plain Coltaine splits the column: he pays the Kherahn Dhobri elders the soldiers' wages (41,000 silver jakatas) and the Wickans add their own wealth; the Kherahn give the refugees passage and escort to Balahn. Duiker, Nil, Nether and the youngest Wickans take the refugees on ahead. Corporal List stays behind. [H for the split; 1 for the battle names]
- **Balahn, Aren Way, the Barrow.** Balahn is a squalid village where Aren Way begins. The refugees run the 3 leagues past old barrows into Aren. Coltaine's last 400 stand on a barrow within sight of the walls. [H]
- **Length.** About 1,500 miles (Wikipedia) or 400 leagues (Rel's message) [1]; Sekala at about 3 months; the whole march perhaps 6 to 9 months [M].

**Alive at the end:** Nil, Nether, Temul, Keneb and family, Blistig and his 300, Squint, the Red Blades, Pullyk, Lenestro, Rel, Gesler's three, and the refugees who reached Aren. **All** of Coltaine's last 400 and **all** of Pormqual's army die. [H]

Wickan clans: Crow, Foolish Dog, Weasel (the Weasels file their teeth after Sekala). [H] The Seti: nothing places them on the Chain; leave them out.

## 3. The enemy

- **Sha'ik.** Killed early (ch. 5) by Lostara Yil after Kalam delivers the Book; Felisin later becomes Sha'ik Reborn in Raraku. [H]
- **Kamist Reloe.** High Mage who faked his death; loses Bat'rol, Sekala and Gelor Ridge; then Dom's mage, weakened because the Path of Hands has fouled the warrens. At the Barrow he blasts the crows to keep them from Coltaine's soul. Survives DG. [H]
- **Korbolo Dom.** Half-Napan Malazan Fist turned renegade; commands from Gelor Ridge on (the Whirlwind Legion, the "Army of the Apocalypse"); survives. [H]
- **Tribes and cities.** Tithansi (Semk-god-ridden; gone after Gelor Ridge); Semk (god destroyed at Sekala); Bhilard, Tregyn, Can'eld (broken at Sanimon); Khundryl (turn at Sanimon); Kherahn Dhobri (neutral, paid). City legions: Guran, Tepasi, Sialk, Ubari. Ubaryd falls during the march. [H]
- **Mallick Rel.** Priest of Mael, secretly a Jhistal; Pormqual's adviser; Coltaine threw him out of Hissar; he delivers Pormqual's army to Dom. [H]

## 4. Aren

- **Pormqual** pulled Admiral Nok's fleet back to Aren, then arrested Nok to keep it in harbour; refuses to sally for Coltaine. [H]
- **Coltaine's death.** The last 400 hold a barrow on Aren Way; Bult, List and Lull fall; Bent is speared. Coltaine is crucified alive; Reloe blasts the crows; Blistig orders Squint to end it; the arrow takes him in the forehead; the crows cover the body and it is gone. Watching from a tower: Duiker, Nil, Nether, Pormqual, Rel, Blistig. [H]
- **The sortie.** Rel talks Pormqual into a dawn attack; the Red Blades are locked up; Pormqual means to arrest Duiker, Nil and Nether. Dom feigns a rout, draws the army into a basin and surrounds it; Rel talks Pormqual into surrender; a warchief beheads Pormqual. Over a day and a half about 10,000 soldiers are crucified along Aren Way, Duiker last at the north end. Dom never takes the city: Blistig's men stay on the walls. [H]
- **Tavore's** fleet is under a week out when the book ends. [H]

- **Felisin, Heboric, Baudin.** Unta (the Cull), Skullcup otataral mine, the desert, the coast (Kulp and the *Ripath*), the *Silanda* wrecked in a warren, Raraku. Kulp and Baudin die; Felisin becomes Sha'ik Reborn. All in Raraku, nowhere near the Chain. [H]
- **Fiddler, Crokus, Apsalar (later Rellock).** Ehrlitan, Pust's temple, Raraku on the Path of Hands, Tremorlor (during the Vathar and Dry March chapters), the Deadhouse and Gothos, out by Shadow. [H]
- **Kalam.** Ehrlitan, Raraku (delivers the Book), the Orbala Odhan (rescues Keneb's family), the Imperial Warren, Aren (around Gelor Ridge), the *Ragstopper*, Malaz City. Apt rescues 1,300 children Dom crucified. [H]
- **Mappo and Icarium.** Raraku, a First Empire ruin, Tremorlor. [H]
- **Path of Hands.** Soletaken and D'ivers converge on Raraku for a promised gate; they block and foul the warrens; Pust's false trail sends them to Tremorlor. Named: Ryllandaras, Gryllen, Messremb, Mogora. [H]
- **The *Silanda*** (Gesler, Stormy, Truth): a burned wreck sailing through Kurald Emurlahn; reaches the Vathar, then Aren. [H]
- **Fener torn down** into the mortal world by Heboric's otataral-tainted touch. [1]

## 6. Calendar

DG starts a few months after GotM and runs mostly alongside MoI [1]. The wiki dates Felisin's Cull to 1162 BS [1] (game rule: no Burn's Sleep year in text). Season of the Chain not confirmed: hot, dry country [M].

## 7. For game systems

- **Wickans:** light horse archers; Foolish Dog heavy cavalry; cattle-dogs fight beside the Weasel Clan. [H]
- **Wickan warlocks** work with land spirits: the spirits devour the Semk god; a fog and a tunnel that holds water; a horse's life spent to power a charge; a ritual at a dead oasis that pulls them into Tellann. [H]
- **Fouled warrens.** The Path of Hands fouls the region's warrens, so mages are weaker. [1] Trygalle carriage runs the warrens; Rel uses Mael and Ruse. [1]
- **Munitions:** Moranth cussers, at least 55 at Sekala; hidden charges at Gelor Ridge; scarce, no resupply. [H]
- **Otataral** (Skullcup) cancels magic. **Dryjhna**: the Apocalypse foretold in the Book of Dryjhna. [H] The Whirlwind's wall of sand round Raraku [M].

## 8. Hard limits for the squad

1. Hissar and Sialk fall.
2. Bat'rol, Sekala, Gelor Ridge and Sanimon are won and the Vathar is lost as canon has it: about 20,000 refugee dead, the nobles' secret deal, Sormo's death.
3. The Wickan warlocks destroy the Semk god.
4. The Trygalle delivery happens; Duiker gets the bottle.
5. The Khundryl turn on Dom.
6. Coltaine's crucifixion and Squint's shot; Bult, List, Lull and all of the last 400 die. A squadmate who stays in the rearguard dies with it; survivors must go on with Duiker's refugees.
7. Pormqual refuses to sally, then sorties; he is beheaded; 10,000 crucified on Aren Way; Duiker last.
8. Nobody unmasks Rel before Keneb, and nobody saves Duiker.
9. No meeting Felisin, Kalam, Fiddler's group, Mappo, Icarium or the Path of Hands in Tremorlor, Raraku or the Imperial Warren.
10. Nok's arrest, the Red Blades' imprisonment and Tavore's arrival after the book stand.
11. Reloe, Dom, Rel, Pullyk and Nethpara are not killed early.

The squad's own story lives in the gaps between battles: raids, scouting, bringing in refugee columns, water, minor tribal fights on the Dry March.

## Chapter 2 re-check (Sun Oct 11, 2026 7:56 PM PDT)

Read for Chapter 2 (Dryj Spring to the Sekala). The Reactor re-read pages came back empty; everything below is from the Malazan Wiki (DG chapter pages 7, 8, 10, 12; Battle of Sekala Crossing; Duiker; Red Blades). Marks as above.

**Confirmed or added**
- **Dryj Spring.** Coltaine left the refugees at the spring to draw water and led the Wickans "over a league" into the Odhan, where, seven to one against, they destroyed the Tithansi; the tribesmen began to see the Wickans as "something more than human". The spring was pillaged after the column left. [1]
- **Sekala, timing.** The army crossed overnight, the cattle in the morning, the refugees in the afternoon; then the rearguard withdrew and the ford was blown. A rest day followed; Reloe had to go north to the next crossing at Sekal, 10 to 11 days. [1]
- **Sekala, people.** Captains Mincer (sappers), **Lull (rearguard, east bank)** and **Chenned (Seventh troops on the west bank)**; **Baria Setral** led the Red Blades; Cuttle blew the road. Sormo, Nil and Nether hid the ford with sorcery; Nil raised ancient dead on the oxbow island (the dead re-fought old Wickan clan wars); Nether faced the Semk sorcerers. Two of Sormo's young warlocks died. [1]
- **Sekala, forces.** West bank: three Guran heavy-infantry legions to the north, **largely unengaged**; Tithansi lancers; Semk warriors and sorcerers, whom the Red Blades fought hardest. The Semk god burned out many of its own sorcerers. The Weasel Clan was badly mauled and short of horses. [1]
- **After Sekala.** Sormo showed Duiker the Semk god's body in Jaghut ice and gave it to the land's spirits, which tore it apart. News came that **Ubaryd had fallen**, so the column had to go all the way to Aren. Nethpara, Tumlit and Lenestro complained to Duiker that Coltaine had taken their servants. [1]
- **The ford.** The battle page says the sappers paved the muddy ford into an underwater road laced with 55 cussers; one chapter summary says a road "from wagons". The game uses paving stones under the water and keeps the count at 55. [1, sources differ]
- **Red Blades.** Seven Cities natives sworn to the Empire, cavalry in chain (sometimes otataral-linked), slit-visored helms, swords, bows, lances; natives call them Malazan lapdogs. Named: Baria Setral, Orto Setral (commander imprisoned in Aren), Tene Baralta, Lostara Yil, Barathol Mekhar. [1]

**Corrections and open points**
- **Duiker was not with the column between Hissar and the Sekala.** He was separated at the rising, travelled three months in disguise with the rebels (he saw Bat'rol's aftermath from their side) and rejoined at the Sekala. Book Two's Chapter 1 (v4.0) shows him in the camp outside Hissar (defensible: he joined Coltaine's staff there) and **at the Vin'til Basin during Bat'rol (a slip)**. Chapter 2 reconciles it: he rode back across the basin after the battle and was lost; he walks into the Sekala camp in rebel robes. Suggest softening his Bat'rol scene in Chapter 1 if it is ever revised.
- **Which Red Blades go to prison in Aren.** The wiki says Baria Setral's company "served under Fist Coltaine and was lost with him", and that the Red Blades locked up in Aren were **Orto Setral's**. The canon sheet (§2) says the Chain's Red Blades were arrested in Aren. Chapter 7 should check this before it sends Ramin away; Chapter 2 only says he will go back "to the company" when it calls, and leaves which company open.
- Name check for the recruit: **Ramin** (of Ehrlitan) clashes with no canon name found.

Sources: malazan.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Sekala_Crossing, /Deadhouse_Gates/Chapter_7, /Chapter_8, /Chapter_10, /Chapter_12, /Duiker, /Red_Blades.

## Chapter 3 re-check (Sun Oct 11, 2026 9:02 PM PDT)

Read for Chapter 3 (the night raid to Gelor Ridge). Sources: the Malazan Wiki pages *Deadhouse Gates/Chapter 12*, *Chapter 13* and *Battle of Gelor Ridge*. The Reactor re-read was not fetched. Marks as above.

**Confirmed or added**
- **The raid** is "nine days east of the River P'atha". The party: Lull, four of his marines, Duiker ("volunteered"), Nil and another young warlock, and three squads of sappers. List was on the south guard and is not placed on the raid. The sappers set off charges to the north-east as a diversion; a warlock's fog and a water-logged tunnel took the party into the Tithansi camp unseen. [1]
- **Correction to §1.** The marines' crossbows killed the Tithansi warleader and two others. The possessed one was a **Semk tribesman**, "the true warleader". He tore apart an earth spirit the warlocks raised, and the other young warlock was killed by sorcery. Nil fell into a catatonic state. The sappers reported the Semk "just got back up" after two explosions. The raid did **not** kill him. A female marine carried Nil out, and the cattle-dog Bent pulled him to the healers on a travois. [1]
- **Claw weapons.** Duiker found Claw throwing stars and knives in Tithansi bodies and told Lull "a Claw was present". Coltaine was rattled. Sormo said a piece of the Semk god had survived the Sekala inside the tribesman. [1]
- **Water.** Duiker reported water just below the surface in the warlocks' tunnel. By dawn the camp was digging pits all through it. [1]
- **The eve.** Coltaine says they will "go through". The Foolish Dog are formed into heavy cavalry, and Duiker doubts it. Lull and List say they will be at the rear. Chenned and Sulmar report that the sappers have deserted, and Lull doubts it. [1]
- **The field.** A dry lake bed about 2,000 by 4,000 paces with the P'atha through it. Reloe's sappers cut the ridge into a single sandy ramp with high banks and cliffs either side. Three groups, each larger than Coltaine's army:
  - at the top, Reloe with elite Semk and Guran heavy infantry and Can'eld archers;
  - in the northern lake bed, Ubari legions with Sialk and Tepasi cavalry;
  - in the southern lake bed, Tithansi archers, Debrahl cavalry, Halafan and Sialk infantry;
  - hidden behind the lines, Tithansi cavalry under the Semk godling.
  
  Coltaine's "spear": marines at the point, Crow riders as the barbs, about 5,000 Foolish Dog, infantry round the wounded wagons, and the Hissar Guard and the Seventh's cavalry at the rear. **About 50,000 refugees and the cattle stayed on "the Shallows", guarded by the Weasel Clan.** [1]
- **The battle.** The marines hit Reloe's elite and Bult cleared the archers. Nil and Nether led a horse forward, and its life fuelled the uphill charge. The sappers came up out of the ramp's banks, and the charge passed over them while they lay under their shields; most lived. On the lake bed the ring bent under the Ubari legions, the wagons rolled south, and the Hissar Guard lost heavily to the Tepasi and Sialk cavalry. The Weasel Clan and their cattle-dogs then came from the east, destroyed that cavalry and turned on the Ubari. The Foolish Dog charged down the ramp into the southern group. [1]
- **The Shallows.** Earlier that day the Semk godling and his Tithansi lancers attacked the Weasels and the refugees. The Wickans had set the trap, using the refugees as bait. Earth spirits tore the godling apart. Hundreds of refugees were killed or wounded, and the Tithan tribe "ceased to exist". [1]
- **Lull** came back with his face badly wounded by a mace. The eye and the nose are from §1. [1 / H]

**How the game uses it.** In the game's version of the raid, the squad escorts one of the three sapper squads through a smaller cut to the north. This squad makes the diversion. The god-ridden Semk comes after it and gets back up; the squad only holds it off while the sappers drop the roof, and never kills it. Sathi finds a Claw star; Lull's and Duiker's finds stay theirs, and Duiker reports the water. At Gelor the squad's stretch is on the Shallows. Tangle is in the ramp's bank with Mincer's company, and both the charge and the trap belong to others. The godling dies to the earth spirits, seen from a distance.

**Open:** whether Nether was on the raid (the wiki says "Nil and another young warlock", unnamed; the game leaves the second warlock unnamed, ungendered and offstage when they die). Whether Nil and Nether are siblings is not checked, so the game never says so. Korbolo Dom's takeover is told by Bult only.

Sources: malazan.fandom.com/wiki/Deadhouse_Gates/Chapter_12, /Chapter_13, /Battle_of_Gelor_Ridge.

## Chapter 4 re-check (Sun Oct 11, 2026 10:34 PM PDT)

Read for Chapter 4 (the Vathar Crossing). Sources: the Malazan Wiki pages *Deadhouse Gates/Chapter 15*, *Chapter 16*, *Sormo E'nath* and *Nethpara*. The *Battle of Vathar Crossing*, *Gesler*, *Mincer* and *Bungle* pages refused the fetch, and the Reactor re-read of chapters 16 and 17 came back with no text. Marks as above.

**Confirmed or added**
- **Before the river** (ch. 15): Lull inducts a group of servants **bought from the nobles** into the Seventh. Corporal List hurt his foot at Gelor Ridge and has visions of a Jaghut. [1]
- **Order of the day** (ch. 16): List brings word that Dom's forces are sighted; Duiker rides ahead with the **Foolish Dog** to the river. They find the ***Silanda*** anchored near the shore. **Gesler, Stormy and Truth** row upriver to scout (Nether asks them to), see **archers crossing the river on ropes**, are spotted, and come back to report. [1]
- **Coltaine arrives** with the main force; the **badly wounded are loaded onto the Silanda** for Aren. Gesler refuses a promotion in a tense exchange with **Lull**, threatens to punch **Bult**, and **Coltaine punches Gesler** instead. Nil says Gesler's blood shows he has nearly ascended. (The summary says "Gesler's fist is shattered", which reads like a slip; the canon sheet has Coltaine breaking his own hand. The game shows the punch and a bound hand on Coltaine at the parley and says no more.) [1]
- **Dom's offer:** representatives of Dom's army ride in, then nobles; they offer to let the refugees cross; Coltaine refuses over the nobles' objections. **Nethpara and Pullyk Alar** pressed him to accept. [1]
- **The deal:** a messenger tells Coltaine and Duiker that a secret deal has been made and the refugees have **started crossing** without leave. Coltaine orders **the sappers to disguise themselves and mingle with the refugees**. **Floating bridges** come to the crossing; archers and pikemen on them begin the slaughter. [1]
- **Sormo E'nath** "fell pierced with arrows while attempting to stop the massacre"; later books add an archer's arrow through his neck. Duiker sees it on **the far bank**. His soul is taken from the water by **an enormous swarm of yellow butterflies**. He carried the spirits of **eight elder warlocks**. [1]
- **The refugees** climb onto the floating bridges and destroy them. A horde charges into the woods after more archers. [1]
- **Duiker and Nethpara:** Duiker grabs Nethpara and starts to choke him and has to be **forcibly pulled off** (one summary: he is choked himself and blacks out). Afterwards Nethpara gets fresh horses for the nobles' carriages and the nobles try to leave the other refugees behind, until Duiker stops them. [1]
- About **20,000 refugees** die. The Council is "ad hoc", "petty and self-centred". [1]

**How the game uses it.** The squad comes down with the main column. The marines' sighting of the ropes is theirs; the squad is one of the squads Lull sends upriver afterwards to cut them. The squad's worst-hurt can go aboard the *Silanda* with the wounded before the parley (the canon order). The squad sees the punch and the envoys and does nothing at either. It finds the deal the night before, through the Council's Hissari servants and the steward's boat, and takes Dom's safe-conducts to Lull; the column is already going into the river by then, so the Fist still hears it from his own messenger in the morning. Mincer's sappers go into the crowd in robes, by Coltaine's order, and Tangle may go with them. The squad holds one bridgehead in the ford and sees a bridge go under to the refugees. Sormo's death and the butterflies are seen from the river; Duiker's hands on Nethpara are told by Duiker afterwards ("they pulled me off"). Coltaine's reason for breaking Mincer is never given; the game says only that he was broken that evening and Bungle has the company.

**Invented here:** Dom watching the parley from the far bank (silent, distant; he says nothing). Dom's safe-conduct papers, one per Council house, "and any who cross in its company". The day's name being given by nobody anyone can remember.

**Open:** why Mincer was broken; whether Coltaine or Gesler broke a hand (the game shows only that Coltaine's is bound); exactly when the *Silanda* sailed (the game has her go before the crossing, with the wounded); who pulled Duiker off Nethpara. Napan skin is blue (Dom is half-Napan), noted in his description.

Sources: malazan.fandom.com/wiki/Deadhouse_Gates/Chapter_15, /Chapter_16, /Sormo_E%27nath, /Nethpara.

## Chapter 5 re-check (Sun Oct 11, 2026 11:54 PM PDT)

Read for Chapter 5 (Vathar Forest and the Dry March). Sources: the Malazan Wiki pages *Deadhouse Gates/Chapter 19*, *Karpolan Demesand* and *List*. *Chapter 17* on the wiki is Kalam and Felisin only, and the Reactor re-read of chapters 18 and 19 came back with no text. Marks as above.

**Confirmed or added**
- **After the Vathar** (ch. 19): about 20,000 refugees and 700 soldiers dead; the Foolish Dog fewer than 500; Sormo dead. Mincer is broken and **recommends Bungle** as his replacement, and Coltaine follows the recommendation. (Chapter 4 already has Mincer broken the evening after; the game leaves the reason open.) [1]

**Invented here:** the Bhilard raiders in the forest mounds, the carter and his son; the Weasel captain's spare grey; the Tregyn water-carrier; the house mark of Haddat's mother's water-sellers on a Tregyn's skin; the shareholder with the frost in his beard and the woman who counted the horses (eleven at Darujhistan, nine in the camp); Ibbin, Ressan's jar-boy; List's ghost saying goodbye at the edge of the forest.

**Open:** where Dom's water comes from (the game implies Ilhar keeps some of it, and says no more); what the bottle is for; how many shareholders died on the way (the game does not say); the exact number of days on the Nenoth (the game says "days").

## Chapter 6 re-check (Mon Oct 12, 2026 1:52 AM PDT)

Read for Chapter 6 (Sanimon to the Geleen Plain). Sources: the Malazan Wiki pages *Deadhouse Gates/Chapter 19*, *Chapter 20*, *Khundryl*, *Coltaine* and *Sulmar*. *Kherahn Dhobri* refused the fetch, and the Reactor re-read of chapters 20 and 21 came back with no text. Marks as above.

**Confirmed or added**
- **Sanimon** (ch. 19; the wiki's summary does not use the name, but it is the battle the day after the Trygalle). Coltaine attacks the **Bhilard and Tregyn at dawn**, before they stir from their camps, and **cannot punch through**; Dom's forces push the rearguard back. Then the **Khundryl** enter: the southern mass charges into the Tregyn lines, the northern mass hits Dom's flank, and a third force strikes the Bhilard from the south valley. Dom's Semk, Can'eld and Tithansi allies are shattered. **Dom spends four hours driving back the Khundryl**; then his force is shattered and the Bhilard and Tregyn are set to flight. [1]
- **The bottle.** "Coltaine flings the bottle he received from Karpolan at Duiker" during the battle: he would rather choose the manner of his death, and someone must live to tell it. [1] (Corrects §1's "which Coltaine gives to Duiker" in timing only: it is thrown, at Sanimon.)
- **The war chief.** The Khundryl war chiefs parley because they "have long awaited the day" it would be decided which southern tribe was strongest. Coltaine replies that the Wickans are beyond such tribal concerns, being part of the Malazan Empire. **Near dusk** the war chief, scoffing at Coltaine's congratulations, raises his sword and shouts *"The Wickans! The Wickans! The Wickans!"* **He is not named** (Warchief Gall belongs to *House of Chains*); the game leaves him unnamed. [1]
- **The Khundryl** gather "by the tens of thousands"; war chiefs wear greyish, almost reptilian armour under skins, furs and fetishes; black hair (moustaches, beards, braids); angular sun-darkened faces, paler than most Seven Cities peoples; tulwars; ponies and horses. **No face paint is mentioned at Sanimon.** [1]
- **The running battle** (ch. 20): after "the debacle at Sanimon", Dom attacks constantly; the running battle lasts **over forty hours**; "only five thousand fighting men and women remain". [1] Tathimon and Dojal Spring are not named in the summaries read.
- **The split** (ch. 20): Coltaine splits the column. **Duiker** leads the refugees to Aren with **Nil and Nether** and "a troop of the youngest Wickan soldiers"; the rest stay behind to hold back the Army of the Apocalypse. "The Wickan children are all the army has left." **The Wickan youths are outraged** to be sent away. Duiker says goodbye to Lull, Coltaine and others. **Corporal List sends word through Lull** that he is staying: *"I have found my war."* Duiker's nameless marine gives him a scrap of cloth to read later. [1]
- **The Kherahn Dhobri** had held back from the fighting; Coltaine gambled they would honour their treaties. **Two tribal elders** agreed to passage for payment, and to provide food, healing and an escort to Balahn. At camp the Kherahnal comfort the refugees, "who have forgotten how to accept kindness". [1] (The 41,000 silver jakatas and the Wickans' added wealth are from §1.)
- **The nobles:** **Tumlit has died unexpectedly** before the split; afterwards Nethpara and Pullyk reveal a separate deal with the Kherahnal for horses to ride ahead, and Duiker forbids it (that belongs to Chapter 7's road). [1]
- **Sulmar** is last mentioned just before Sanimon; his death is not confirmed. Chenned is not on the pages read. [1]

**How the game uses it.** The squad goes in at dawn with the Seventh against the Tregyn and the Bhilard, cannot break them, and holds a fold of the hill at noon (a heat fight) until the Khundryl come down; it sees the bottle thrown from the standard (never touches it) and the war chief's salute at dusk, with Haddat translating some of the parley. A Khundryl warrior can paint one of the squad with three strokes of ochre and ash (**the Khundryl Paint**, a trick: the paint is the game's invention, since the wiki mentions none at Sanimon; the artist's brief also asks for "painted"). Dom's water-carts at the field's edge are the game's own, and so is Ilhar there. The running retreat is a montage: Tathimon is a name in the dark, Dojal Spring a fight for the column's water against a company of Dom's (both invented in detail). On the Geleen Plain the squad guards the elders' chests against Can'eld (invented), sees the bargain made, hears List's message from Lull, and meets the angry Wickan children. The split is played as the squad's: the Fist sends the squads that hold stretches on with their stretches, "not an order for anyone who'd rather stay"; the sergeant always goes (Bult refuses the sergeant outright).

**Invented here:** Dom's water-carts at Sanimon; the Khundryl warrior and the paint; the Can'eld at the silver; Dom's badgeless captain at Dojal Spring; the Kherahn old woman who judges the chests, and the Kherahn healers the squad's silver buys; Bungle's rumour that "sappers dig the last ditch" (Mincer singing in it); the Red Blades riding on with the column to Aren (Ramin's commander); the Crow girl who carried two city girls "as far as the river".

**Open:** whether Baria Setral's Red Blades stayed with Coltaine (the wiki says his company "was lost with him") or went on to Aren (the canon sheet has the Chain's Red Blades arrested in Aren); the game has Ramin's company go on to Aren, and Ramin may stay with the rearguard himself if asked or argued. Mincer's and Bungle's fates (the game says only that Bungle's company means to stay; Cuttle is never named in it). Where exactly Tathimon and Dojal Spring lie. Whether the youngest Wickans included riders as old as seventeen (the game says yes: Sathi is ordered to go).
