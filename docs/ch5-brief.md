## What Chapter 5 is

Whiskeyjack has learned what the dust-line on the Rhivi Plain was: Adjunct Lorn and a T'lan Imass, and they are in the Gadrobi Hills east of the city digging for something. He sends the Fourth out to watch the hills — "You found her once without meaning to. Find her again on purpose. Watch. Don't be seen. If she sees you, you're dead, and so is the mission, so be dead somewhere else." The Bridgeburners cannot go themselves; Kalam and Quick Ben have their own business (Kalam is following Lorn in canon; do not put him on-screen with the Fourth — he is ahead of them and they never catch up).

Canon fixed points, all *near* the Fourth, none changed by them: **Adjunct Lorn** (a hard woman with an otataral sword that deadens magic — Tuft feels it as a hole in the world and cannot use Meanas within a hundred paces of her; use that) and **Onos T'oolan, "Tool"** (a T'lan Imass: a thing of bone, hide and dried sinew with a flint sword, who has been dead three hundred thousand years and talks slowly and precisely and is, in his way, kind) find and open the barrow of the Jaghut Tyrant, Raest. Tool breaks the wards; something under the hill begins, very slowly, to wake — nobody sees it this chapter; the Fourth feels the ground get *interested* in them. Lorn and Tool leave for the city. **Captain Paran** and **Toc the Younger** ride into the hills to meet the Adjunct (Toc has one eye and knows Ellis; Paran is a man who has died and come back and it shows). **Hairlock** — the mad mage in a puppet's body, a marionette the size of a child with a painted face — arrives on the night the barrow is opened, hunting Paran; he opens a rent in the world (a warren gate, Chaos-smelling), and **Toc is pulled through it and is gone** (he is not dead — he turns up years later in another book — but nobody here knows that). Then the **Hounds of Shadow** come through the rent, summoned by Quick Ben's ritual from the city, and tear Hairlock apart; the puppet's strings go slack; the Hounds leave. Paran lives. Paran rides for the city at dawn to find Whiskeyjack, and the Fourth is told to ride ahead of him. **Crone** passes over the hills. **Sethand** and a Rhivi party are watching the hills too (they carry something wrapped with great care and do not say what — see Chapter 2). Nobody canon dies or lives because of the player. Toc goes through the rent no matter what.

**The choice that matters.** The rent Toc went through stays open a few breaths after him, a tear of grey light on a hillside, and it *pulls*. If Ellis is in the squad, she is already moving: "He's my captain. Sergeant. He's my captain." **Through** = let her go (`S.f.c5_key='through'`): she goes into the grey and the rent closes on her heel; `unrecruit('ellis')` (the engine removes her from the squad and sets `S.f.ellisGone=1`, keeping her loyalty record); Brisk −1 ("we don't leave people; you just did"), Tuft +1 (she saw the rent and understood that someone had to), Ohl +1 ("some lists close from the other side"), Kettle −1. `S.f.c5_ellisThrough=1` — she may come back in Chapter 7; leave that open. **Hold** = stop her (`S.f.c5_key='hold'`): Might 13 to physically hold her, or Brisk does it without being asked if `S.loy.brisk >= 2` (no check); she does not forgive it this chapter: `loy('ellis',-3)`, `S.f.c5_ellisHeld=1`, and she says one sentence at dawn that is the right one. If Ellis is **not** in the squad, the choice is Tuft's: the rent is *open*, Meanas is howling through it, Tuft wants to step through "just to the threshold, Sergeant, I can feel where he went." **Through** = let her (`S.f.c5_key='through'`): she goes to the threshold and comes back — after the fight, at the end of the Hounds sequence — with grey in her hair that was not there before and `S.f.c5_tuftMarked=1` (Shadow has noticed her; plant, don't resolve), Tuft +2, Ohl −1, Brisk −1. **Hold** = refuse her: Tuft −2, Brisk +1; `S.f.c5_key='hold'`. In both versions the choice node is the same node id (`c5_rent`) with the text branching on `SQUAD().includes('ellis')`. On both paths the survive fight follows immediately.

**Fights.** (1) `barrow_wards` — when Tool breaks the outer wards, things come up out of the smaller barrows on the ridge where the Fourth is hiding: three `wight` (already defined in the engine from Chapter 2 — do NOT redeclare it) and one `ward` (a Jaghut construct of stone and ice, new), ordinary, xp 220, `open:true`, `dark:true`. Kettle's cusser is finally used here — a node before the fight offers it (if `S.inv.cusser > 0`: `S.inv.cusser -= 1` and `startBattle('barrow_wards', {pre:true})` — the engine's `pre` option makes the opening sharper hit everything for 1d10; use it, it is already implemented). (2) `the_rent` — survive 3 rounds on the hillside while `shade`s (existing foe) and a `warrenspawn` (new) pour out of the rent; wave at round 2: two more shades, text: "Something is coming through behind them. Something with too many legs, and then, behind *that*, the sound of dogs."; `after` node: the Hounds come through — two shapes the size of horses — go *past* the Fourth without looking, and take Hairlock apart on the next hill; xp 240. If `S.f.c5_key==='through'` and Ellis went, the party has five; the engine handles the squad size.
